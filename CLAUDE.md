## Find Your Match (FYM) is the top project. These are hard rules.
Live app: claude.ai artifact 6epA9HuEdT32zUp79Qv3kd. Its published index.html (`const D={...}`) is the build source of truth.

1. **Gate before every publish.** Run `python3 builder/fym_gate.py <build_dir>`. If it fails, do not publish. No exceptions.
2. **Independent QA before Tiffany sees it.** A separate agent that did not build the change tests phone (390px) and desktop: both doors (tiles first, mat first), color filter (switch + clear), zoom persists across swipes, card + share, and VIEWS tile and mat photos via contact sheets. Tiffany is never the QA team.
3. **Album tile photos are canonical.** Every set with a match album uses its album photo `assets/tiles/album/<set>.*` (map: scripts/swipe/canon_tiles.py). Default framing shows a tile back/layers + faces.
4. **"Tiffany's match" only from her real picks** (album or swipe-book yes). Catalog sets: `m=[]`. The mat-door "Tiffany matched this mat with N sets" counts only those.
5. **Locks.** `builder/fym_flags/locks.json` holds approved photos/crops/names/match lists. Catalog scrapes and refreshes may only ADD to a pending queue; run `builder/fym_apply_locks.py`; "changed locked items" must be 0.
6. **New products** become visible only after their photo passes the flags (`builder/fym_flags/*_photo_flags.json`). **FYM photo bar is LOW (Tiffany, Oct 21):** the photo only has to show that it's a mat and let you colour-match it. Tiles, racks or props on the mat, or a room around it, are fine as long as the mat and its colours are clearly visible. Hide only when you can't see the mat or its colours (packaging, a close-up of a corner, a different product). The stricter bar (a clean shot that crops into a match card) applies only to pulling a mat into a match ALBUM. Tiles must show tiles. **Added Tiffany, Oct 21 8:03am:** (1) DISTANCE: a shot from too far away is out even if you can see the dominant colour, because subtle colours get lost (flag `too_far`). (2) SOLID / VEGAN LEATHER mats get a lower bar: they're uniform, so even a partly rolled or angled shot shows the colour. (3) WHEN IN DOUBT, PULL IT: hide it and put it on her 'Questionable for FYM' review list, `builder/fym_flags/fym_questionable.json` (key/img, name, brand, reason, photo path, optional candidate); better a great first impression than a bad photo. Pattern hidden under tiles/racks = flag `covered`; gate [3] keeps `too_far`/`covered`/`questionable` hidden and [3d] fails if a Questionable item is visible. Before hiding, check every brand image (curl `/products/<handle>.json`) for a closer/clearer one. (4) Paige Spearin x Tigre de Tartan collabs earn NO commission (TdT excluded them): they stay in the library, but don't go out of our way to add or promote them (e.g. never bring a Paige Spearin mat into Holiday; gate [3d]).
