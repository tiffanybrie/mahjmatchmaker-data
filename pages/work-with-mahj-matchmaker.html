Work with Mahj Matchmaker

I'm Tiffany. I match mahjong tiles with the mats and racks that make them sing, so players can build a table they love. Here's how brands and I work together.

Find Your Match, launching late October

Find Your Match is a new swipe-to-match tool on mahjmatchmaker.com. Players start with the tiles they own or the mat they love, swipe through mats and racks that pair with it, and save a match card to share with their group. Your products show up with your shop links and discount code, so a player who finds your mat can buy it in one tap.

Ways we work together

Affiliate partnership

Your brand gets its own discount code page, a spot on the Discount Codes page, and your products in Find Your Match. Players use code MAHJMATCH.

Product photos

Clean product photos (mats flat, racks straight on, tile sets in a pile) get your products into Find Your Match and my tile set albums. I'll send you an upload link.

New releases

Tell me what's coming. I can match new tile sets, mats and racks as they launch, so players see them with pairings on day one.

About product samples

A first-hand review means I've held it, used it or played with it. Without a sample, I won't give a product a first-hand review on my blog. That doesn't mean it will never be recommended here based on other criteria, like how well it matches.

Sending a product sample doesn't guarantee a feature or a positive review. You'll always get my honest opinion.
