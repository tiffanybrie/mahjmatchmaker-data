Style of Your Life, the album set

Your next album after Juniper, built the Juniper way. It’s the set that looks like Juniper: green backs with a pink-and-green face palette. The one question for you is her best features (pick 4 faces). The match order is Claude’s draft until you OK it.

Why this set. On Oct 28 you put My Fair Mahjong’s Style of Your Life first in the album order and said it looked like the green-on-green set Oh My Mahjong was teasing, which turned out to be Juniper. None of My Fair Mahjong’s other sets is close: the Park Series is mint melamine and Crystal Confetti Citrus Green is clear-backed lime.

How close it really is. Both sets have green backs and white faces with pink and green art. The differences:

Juniper (Oh My Mahjong)Style of Your Life (My Fair Mahjong)
BackForest Green Jade, darker, jade-look stonePearl green with a malachite-style swirl, brighter and more emerald
LayersChartreuse + tealOne deep green layer
FacesHot pink, coral, mint, tealIndigo (the main ink), wine, bubblegum pink, cornflower, marigold
ThemeChinoiserie gardenA day of shopping: shoes, handbags, jewelry, escalators

Mats that would top both albums: pink-and-green mats (Palm Royale, Cherry Blossom Jade, Peonies Burst, Botanical Jewel) and the deep greens (Garden Glow – Emerald Green, Green Malachite). To keep the two albums apart, none of Juniper’s top 7 is in this album. Only 3 of these 22 mats are also in Juniper, and they sit at the bottom under “Also in Juniper” for you to keep or cut.

How this album leans its own way: navy and indigo (Tennis Club, Peacock Circle, Floral Instincts, the navy group), wine (Crimson Harvest), and cornflower and marigold (MOD Daisies). Juniper leans on chartreuse, teal and coral. Different tile racks too: My Fair Mahjong Indigo Blue + Magenta here, Oh My Mahjong Hot Pink + Agate on Juniper.

Her colors + color theory

Clean and bright. My Fair Mahjong calls it a pearl green back with a deep green middle layer and a white face. The back swirls from near-black green to a pale pearly mint, so it reads brighter and more emerald than Juniper.

Indigo is her main ink. More of her face art is indigo than any other color (numbers, jewelry stands, joker arches). Navy mats pick it up, which no other green album does.

Complementary. Her pink and wine sit across the wheel from her greens, so pink and red mats make her pop. Marigold and cornflower are her small accents (earrings, chains, escalators).

Mat colors that should work, strongest first:

  • Navy + pink + green together: all of her at once (Tennis Club, Floral Instincts, Peach Petals).
  • Her faces as a print: cornflower, pink, red and marigold on green (MOD Daisies).
  • Wine + pink on deep green: moody, and good through the holidays (Crimson Harvest).
  • Cobalt or navy grounds: her indigo, with the backs as the contrast (Peacock Circle, Color Wave – Cobalt Center, Grandmillennial Peony).

Use with care: chartreuse, lime, teal and coral mats (those are Juniper’s colors and she has none), and earthy olive or tan greens.

Tile racks: My Fair Mahjong Indigo Blue + Magenta, the same solid acrylic rack in two colors, both 20″. My Fair Mahjong’s own favorites with this set are tortoiseshell, magenta, teal, macaron blue, pearl white and clear. Malachite Dapple was the obvious choice, but it’s black with a neon green dapple (not her pearly green), and the only photo is a stack, not a single rack.
