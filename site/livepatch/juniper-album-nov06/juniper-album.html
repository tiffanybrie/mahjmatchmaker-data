Her colors + color theory

Clean, not earthy. The back is dark, but its color, Forest Green Jade, is a deep jewel green with a jade-look stone pattern, not a muddy or dusty one. The layers (chartreuse, teal) and faces (hot pink, coral, mint, gold) are all clear, saturated colors. Deep isn’t earthy: she sits with Emerald and Jasmine, not with the earthy sets.

A cool base, warm accents. Forest green (140°) and teal (172°) are cool; chartreuse (74°) leans warm. The warmth comes from the faces: coral (5°) and gold (44°). Her hot pink (330°) is a cool, blue-leaning pink.

Complementary. Pink sits straight across the wheel from her greens. That’s why a pink-and-green mat makes her pop (a pink center, like Palm Royale, gives the most contrast), and why a mat that’s all green reads tone on tone (moody, but the faces do the work).

Light and dark. The wheel shows light colors toward the center and dark ones toward the rim, with the full, clear color in between. That’s why Forest Green Jade sits out near the edge, on dark green, while her hot pink and coral sit closer in.

Mat colors that should work, strongest first:

  • A pink center inside kelly green: the most contrast, her faces and layers at once (Palm Royale, Peonies Burst, Cherry Blossom Jade).
  • Deep forest or emerald ground + pink florals: back and faces in one (Garden Glow, Botanical Jewel).
  • Lime or chartreuse centers: the layer, turned up (Palm Beach Tropical Pink Lime, Zesty Plume).
  • Pink lotus and chinoiserie gardens: the art on her flowers (Lotus Cove, the Sophie Clima pagodas).
  • Tone-on-tone emerald and stone: moody, the faces bring the pink (Emerald Cove, Green Malachite).

Use with care: aqua- or blue-led mats (she has no aqua), lilac and purple borders, and earthy sage, olive or tan greens (they read muddy next to her clean jewel tones).

Odd in the photos: Oh My Mahjong’s single-tile shot runs brighter than the pile shot (teal reads aqua, chartreuse reads lime), so we use the pile values. The back isn’t one flat color: its jade-look stone pattern runs from near-black to mid green, so match mats to its deep end. The “red” dragon is pink on this set.
