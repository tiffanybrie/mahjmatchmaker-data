Mahj it Pretty

3.1K members · pretty tables, mats and tiles · no promo rule listed

Hi everyone! I'm Tiffany. I'm a graphic designer who fell hard for mahj, and honestly the tiles and mats are half the reason.

Every time a new mat came out, I'd try to picture my tiles on it, and I ended up with about 10,000 screenshots on my phone. So I built a little tool for it: Find Your Mat-ch™. Pick your tiles (or your mat) and it shows you matches side by side, from lots of different brands. It's free: mahjmatchmaker.com/find-your-match

You all have the best eye for a pretty table, so I'd love for you to try it and tell me what you'd change!
