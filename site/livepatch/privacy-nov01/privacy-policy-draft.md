_Last updated: November 2026_

Mahj Matchmaker (mahjmatchmaker.com) is run by Tiffany Emery in Louisville, Kentucky. This page explains what we collect and why.

## Find Your Mat-ch™ accounts
If you choose to sign in, we ask for your **email address only**. We use it to send you a sign-in code or link and to keep your stars and saved match cards on every device. We don't send marketing email from your sign-in, and we never sell or share your email. Sign-in is handled by our provider, Supabase.

You can use Find Your Mat-ch without an account. Stars then stay only on your own device.

## Shopping links and codes
Many links on this site are affiliate links (including ShopMy and brand affiliate programs). If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The brand or link service may use cookies to credit the sale; their own privacy policies apply.

## Analytics
We use basic, anonymous usage counts (for example, which tile sets are searched most) to improve the site and the app.

## Your choices
To delete your account and saved stars, email mahjmatchmaker@gmail.com and we'll remove it.
