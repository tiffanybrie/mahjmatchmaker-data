# Privacy Policy

**Effective November 1, 2026**

Hi, I'm Tiffany Emery, and Mahj Matchmaker is my little corner of the internet for matching mahjong tiles, mats and racks. I'm based in Louisville, Kentucky. This page explains, in plain words, what information this site and the Find Your Mat-ch™ app collect, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email me at mahjmatchmaker@gmail.com.

## The short version
- You can browse the site and use Find Your Mat-ch without giving me any personal information.
- If you join my email list, sign in to Find Your Mat-ch, or send me a message, I keep only what you send me and use it only for that.
- I don't sell or share your personal information, and I never will.
- Many shopping links on this site are affiliate links, so I may earn a commission when you buy. Details are below.

## What I collect, and why

**Email list.** If you sign up through a newsletter form, I keep your email address (and your name if you give it) so I can send you Mahj Matchmaker emails, like new match albums and partner discount codes. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

**Messages.** If you use the contact form, I receive your name, your email address and your message, and I use them only to reply to you.

**Find Your Mat-ch accounts.** Signing in is optional. If you sign in, I collect your email address so I can send you a one-time sign-in code or link, and I save your stars and saved match cards to your account so they're there on any device. Sign-in is run by my provider, Supabase. I don't use your sign-in email for marketing unless you also join my email list.

**Stars without an account.** If you don't sign in, your stars are saved only in your own browser on your own device. They never reach me.

**Site visits.** My site runs on Squarespace, which records standard visit information such as pages viewed, the site you came from, your general location (city or region), and your browser and device type. I use these totals to see which pages and matches people enjoy. I don't use Google Analytics, a Facebook/Meta pixel, a Pinterest tag or other advertising trackers on this site.

**Spam protection.** Forms on this site use Google reCAPTCHA (through Squarespace) to keep out bots. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to it.

## Cookies
Squarespace places a few cookies so the site works (for example, keeping forms secure) and for its built-in visit statistics. Signing in to Find Your Mat-ch stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in. You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; the site and app will still work, but you may need to sign in again and stars saved only on that device may be lost.

## Affiliate links
I'm paid when you buy through many of the links on this site, at no extra cost to you. That's how Mahj Matchmaker stays free. These include partner brands' discount codes and links (like code MAHJMATCH), ShopMy links, and Amazon links. When you click one, the brand, ShopMy or Amazon may use cookies to credit the sale to me, and their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Brands are never paid placements in my color matching. The best match is the best match.

## Who helps me run things
- **Squarespace:** website hosting, forms, email list and visit statistics.
- **Supabase:** sign-in and saved stars for Find Your Mat-ch accounts.
- **jsDelivr:** delivers the app's images and code quickly. It sees standard request information (like your IP address) the way any website server does.
- **Google reCAPTCHA:** spam protection on forms.
- **ShopMy, Amazon and partner brands:** only when you click their links.
- **Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest:** if you follow me or join my Facebook group, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

## How long I keep things
I keep your email list sign-up until you unsubscribe, contact messages for as long as I need them to help you, and your Find Your Mat-ch account until you ask me to delete it.

## Your choices
- **Unsubscribe** from my emails any time using the link in any email.
- **See, correct or delete** your information, including your Find Your Mat-ch account and saved stars: email mahjmatchmaker@gmail.com and I'll take care of it, usually within a few days.
- **Do Not Sell or Share:** I don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia or other states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights to access or delete your data; just email me and I'll honor them.

## Kids
Mahj Matchmaker is meant for adults. It isn't directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given me information, email me and I'll delete it.

## Changes
If I change how I handle your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
