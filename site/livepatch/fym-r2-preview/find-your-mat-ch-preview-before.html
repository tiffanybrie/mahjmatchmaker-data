Questions, answered

Who picks the matches?

Tiffany’s picks start off, so you swipe the whole library. Her own matches for your set still lead the deck, marked Tiffany’s match. Want only hers? Tap Tiffany’s picks to turn them on.

I already have a mat. Can I start there?

Yes. Tap Start with a mat and swipe through tile sets that match it, then add racks.

How do I save?

Use code MAHJMATCH to save at 30+ partner brands. Your match card links every piece, and each brand’s code page has the details.

Does it cost anything?

No. Find Your Mat-ch is free, and you don’t need an account to use it. Want your hearts and match cards on every device? Sign in with just your email.
