Where it all began

Mahj Matchmaker started when I fell in love with mahjong and couldn’t stop mocking up tiles, mats and racks to find my perfect first set. Those first 700+ mock-ups became the original tile library. Today there are over 900 matches, and counting, and new albums live right here on the site.
