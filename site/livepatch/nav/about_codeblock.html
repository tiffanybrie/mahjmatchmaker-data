How Mahj Matchmaker started

Mahj Matchmaker started a bit by accident, when I absolutely fell in love with the game of mahjong and realized I was completely obsessed with curating the perfect first set for myself. Cue the rabbit hole of research and a ridiculous amount of mahj photos saved to my phone.

I realized I needed to use my skills as a graphic designer to mock all these options up and figure out what I liked best. And I guess you could say I just never stopped! I’ve always loved playing with color and pattern, and mahjong tiles are full of both.

Those first 700+ mock-ups became the original Mahj Matchmaker library: big Mahj Matchmaker albums and the tile library that lived in our original Facebook group, Mahj Matchmaker: Hooking Up Tiles, Mats & Racks. Our library outgrew Facebook’s limits and recently found a new home here on mahjmatchmaker.com, so as many mahj players as possible can find their perfect set. Today there are over 900 matches, and counting.

How I make a match

  • Start with the mat

    I find the dominant color in the center of the mat. That’s the color the whole table is built around.

  • Find the pop

    For a color that pops, I jump to its complement on the color wheel and match the tint, so a magenta center gets a bright green.

  • Finish with racks

    For racks that match the tiles, I pull the tile’s layer colors, plus a neutral like tortoise, opal or wood. Never a boring color wall.
