Her colors + color theory

Rich, not earthy. Her back, Mulberry, is a deep cool berry under a glassy acrylic, with a gold-foil art deco palm on every tile. The faces are ivory with clear inks: teal, mulberry, rose, gold, navy and periwinkle. Dark, but clean.

A cool base, warm accents. Mulberry (332°) leans blue, not orange, so she is a cool berry. Her teal bams and green dragon (184°) are cool too. The gold foil (42°) and rose crak frames (14°) bring the warmth.

Complementary. Teal sits almost straight across the wheel from her berry. That’s why a deep teal mat makes her pop (Viola, Caroline in Peacock, the Lagoon Deco Dragon), and why an all-berry mat is berry on berry.

Light and dark. The wheel shows light colors toward the center and dark toward the rim. Mulberry sits out at the dark edge, so she wants a mat that is deep too, or a clean ivory that lets her shine.

Mat colors that should work, strongest first:

  • Deep teal with berry or pink flowers: her back plus her bams (Viola, Caroline in Peacock, Wren).
  • Her sister color, Lagoon: the Deco Dragon (Lagoon) and solid deep teals.
  • Plum and berry florals with a teal or ivory accent (Pretty in Plum, Mulberry and Sage).
  • Art deco fans and gold lines (Tiger Fans, Gilded Peacock).

Use with care: orange-reds, rust and brick (they clash with a cool berry), neon pinks, and mats in the same Mulberry (berry on berry).

Odd in the photos: Mulberry photographs brighter and pinker on loose tiles in the light, and darker and more wine in the box; we matched to the middle, a deep berry.

In Bam Bird Boutique’s words: “two jewel-toned colorways: Lagoon and Mulberry”, “inspired by the bold elegance of the Art Deco era.”
