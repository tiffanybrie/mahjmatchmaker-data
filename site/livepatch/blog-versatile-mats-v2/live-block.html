The mats I reach for again and again, because they work with so many tile sets.

If you're only buying one or two mats, marry the versatile one, then date the rest. You want a mat that plays nicely with the tiles you have now and the tiles you'll fall for next year. These are the ones that keep showing up in my match albums, from lots of different brands.

Leather and leatherette

If you only buy one mat, I'd start here. A solid leather-look mat acts like a neutral, and warm browns flatter wood-look, tortoise and jewel-tone tiles alike.

Brown Vegan Leather by Charleston Mahjong Club

A warm, saddle-brown vegan leather with a soft, worn-in look. It's the mat I'd hand to anyone who owns wood-look or tortoise tiles, and it makes cream tile faces look rich instead of plain.

See it with Gatsby, Cypress, Smith, December 1994, Hummingbird and Sunday.

Vegan Leather by Bam Bird Boutique

Bam Bird makes their vegan leather mat in Caramel, Navy, Gold, Hunter Green and Cream. Caramel is my favorite all-rounder, and Navy is the one I'd pick for jewel-tone tiles.

See it with Drake, Smith, Classic Tortoise and December 1994.

Cognac by Oh My Mahjong

A rich caramel vegan leather that behaves like a true neutral. It isn't in Oh My Mahjong's shop right now, so if you spot one in a buy/sell group, it's a keeper.

See it with Heritage 1.0, Gatsby, Heritage 2.0, Drake, Cypress, Sierra, Smith and Miss Classic Jade.

Mats with lots of colors

A coral center, a lime border and blue florals. Three strong colors means lots of tile backs find something to pick up, from soft pastels to tortoise.

Jardín de Los Jefes by Tigre de Tartan

Bold garden florals in nearly every color on a crisp white ground, framed in rattan. Bright tile sets look right at home here.

Lorelei by Oh My Mahjong

Pagodas and florals in pink, turquoise and soft green. Playful without being loud, and those three color families give almost any tile back something to pick up.

Darjeeling by Oh My Mahjong

Layered pagodas, trees and florals in blues, greens, honey and brown. It works with everything from wood-look tiles to bright sets.

Dark, layered blues and greens

Another kind of mat that matches almost everything: one that's mostly dark, built from lots of neighboring shades. Think navy, cobalt, slate, jade and celery green all together. Because it isn't one flat color, there's always a shade for your tiles to pick up, and brighter tiles really pop against it.

Astor by Oh My Mahjong

Midnight blue florals framed in plaid, with pops of green and pink. There's so much dark, layered color here that bright tiles really pop.

See it with Heritage 1.0, Gatsby, Heritage 2.0, Lagoon, Solara, Gemma, Luminaire and Moonlight.

Jade by Oh My Mahjong

Teal, ocean blue and soft green waves. All those blues and greens blend into one rich backdrop, and almost any tile back stands out against it.

See it with Heritage 1.0, Gatsby, Heritage 2.0, Lagoon, Willow, Cypress, Solara and Coquette.

Retired favorites worth hunting for

Spring Fling, Citrus Songbird and Enchanted by Oh My Mahjong were some of the most versatile mats I've ever matched. They're retired, so if you see one in a mahjong buy/sell group, grab it.

How to pick a mat that works with everything

  • Start with leather or a leather look. Solid, warm neutrals go with almost every tile set.
  • Look for three or more colors. Different tile backs can each pull a different one.
  • Don't fear green. Your bams are green, so earthy greens and natural textures like rattan and basketweave are always in play.
  • Test it in Find Your Mat-ch™! Start with the mat you're eyeing and swipe through the tile sets you own. If it works with all of them, you've found your versatile mat.
