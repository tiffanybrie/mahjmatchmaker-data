The mahjong mats I reach for again and again because they work with so many tile sets.

If you're only going to buy one or two mats, you want ones that play nicely with the tiles you have now and the tiles you'll fall for next year. These are the mats I've found to be the most versatile after matching hundreds of tile and mat combinations. My albums are always growing, so think of this as my hands-on list rather than a final count.

My most versatile Oh My Mahjong mats

1. Darjeeling by Oh My Mahjong

One of my all-time favorites. The pattern and colors are so layered (pagodas, trees, florals, blues, greens, honey and brown) that it works with everything from earthy wood-look tiles like Heritage to bright sets like Dandy.

See it with Heritage, Heritage 2.0, Drake, Sierra, Tortoise, Glow Wild, Lola and Dandy.

2. Jade by Oh My Mahjong

Deep teal and ocean blues with soft green waves. It reads almost like a neutral, so it lets bright tile backs pop without fighting them.

See it with Birdie, Glow Wild, Heritage, Lagoon, Moonlight, Pearlescent, Solara and Willow.

3. Lorelei by Oh My Mahjong

Colorful pagodas and florals in pinks, blues and soft greens. Playful without being loud, and those three color families give almost any tile back something to pick up.

See it with Amara, Birdie, Dandy, Emerald, Glitterville, Lola, Moonlight and Palm Royale.

4. Astor by Oh My Mahjong

Midnight blue florals framed in plaid. Classic and a little preppy, and the multicolor botanicals tie in greens, pinks and blues.

See it with Dandy, Gemma, Heritage, Luminaire, Sierra, Taylor, Solara and Lola.

5. Anastasia by Oh My Mahjong

A pink center framed in turquoise, with layers of florals in between. It is my go-to for pink, coral and pastel tile sets, and the turquoise border keeps it from feeling one-note.

See it with Amara, Dandy, Glitterville, La Fete, Pearlescent, Solara, Sorbet and Taylor.

6. Cognac by Oh My Mahjong

A rich caramel vegan leather that behaves like a true neutral. It is simple, warm and works especially well with wood-look, jewel-tone and darker tile sets.

See it with Heritage, Heritage 2.0, Drake, Jade, Malachite, Sierra and Gatsby.

How to pick a mat that works with everything

  • Look for a mat with three or more colors in it. Mats like Darjeeling, Lorelei and Jardín de Los Jefes mix so many colors that different tile backs can each pull a different one.
  • Natural textures act like neutrals. Rattan, basketweave and leather-look mats like Cognac go with almost everything.
  • Deep backgrounds make tiles pop. Navy and teal mats like Astor and Jade give white tile faces crisp contrast.
  • Start with your tiles. Browse the tile match albums for your set and see which of these mats show up.
