A first look at Find Your Mat-ch

Thank you so much for being a partner brand of Mahj Matchmaker! I'm excited to share a first look at Find Your Mat-ch™, our new matching tool on mahjmatchmaker.com. It launches tomorrow, Tuesday, October 27.

It has truly been a labor of love. Players start with the tiles or the mat they own, swipe through mats and racks across brands, and save a match card to share. Take a first look

We've worked hard to bring in your catalog, so please search for your brand and have a look around. It's brand new and not perfect yet, so I'd love your help getting it just right.

How your photos show up

We pulled your product photos directly from your website. For matching to work, a shopper needs to see your product clearly, so here's what we look for:

  • Tiles: a photo that clearly shows some of the tile faces and their colors, plus the color of the tile backs.
  • Mats: an aerial view of the mat in clear view, without other accessories on it.
  • Racks: a simple horizontal photo of a single rack on a white or solid background works best. Groups of racks and racks on an angle work too, but we can't use racks on mats or on busy backgrounds.

Sometimes we couldn't find a photo that works without distracting the shopper, like a mat almost completely covered in tiles, or a few tiles scattered over a busy mat. Where a photo was good enough to match with, we used it, and for some tile sets we made a compilation image to show the faces and backs together.

You have full creative control over how your products show up in Find Your Mat-ch within these guidelines. If you see a "Photo coming soon" label on an item, we're still finding or creating a photo that works for matching. If you'd like to send us a better photo for any item, we'd love that!

  • Something looks off, or there's a product you'd like us to take down? Tell us with this quick form, and include your brand name.
  • Have a better photo you'd like us to use? Upload it with the second button (Dropbox, no account needed), with the product name in the file name.
