Sierra Mahjong Tile Matches

Sierra has a color palette that is inspired by the desert landscape of Sierra Nevada - burnt orange backs with layers of white, the palest of blues & a rather bright olive green. But her real magic is in her FACES - the gold accents of the cracks, dragons & flowers are BEAUTIFUL in person and hard to capture in a photo. IYKYK with this one!! She is a sleeper and usually in stock but growing in popularity as more and more people see these tiles in person.

To me, these have a vintage vibe, especially in the dragons and flowers. If there's a better solid backed fall/autumn tile I haven't found it yet. Note that filters and flashes can make these tiles look more orange than they actually are. They are definitely orange, but a burnt orange so there's brown added that tones it down a bit (unlike OMM Sunset, who has zero chill:) If you like an earthy vibe, this is a great tile to add some non-bright earthy color. I'll be adding more pairings with brands like Sunday Mahjong, Ozark & more to show you more of her vibe. Everything earthy also works for more masculine tablescapes, the key is to tone down the brightness which this tile does well. Nothing but love for Sierra!
