Jade Mahjong Tile Matches

Miss Mahjong's "Miss Classic" Jade tiles are another gorgeous SOLID back patterned tile, meaning the pattern is all the way thru the acrylic and not a clear back with a photo underneath like some textured sets. Jade and it's sister set Malachite share a lot of similarities but are also distinctly different.

The Jade back itself has much lighter shades of green and while it definitely has movement it is a more gentle marbling than its Malachite counterpart.
