Questions, answered

Who picks the matches?

Tiffany’s picks are built right in. While the button says Tiffany’s picks: on, every mat you see is her own match for your set. Tap it to swipe the whole library and make your own matches.

I already have a mat. Can I start there?

Yes. Tap I have a mat and swipe through tile sets that match it, then add racks.

How do I save?

Use code MAHJMATCH to save at 30+ partner brands. Your match card links every piece, and each brand’s code page has the details.

Does it cost anything?

No. Find Your Mat-ch is free, with nothing to sign up for. Swipe as much as you like.

How it works

Find your match, step by step

Scroll down. Each screen shows exactly what to tap.

  1. 1

    Pick your door

    Start with the tiles you own, or start with the mat you already love.

  2. 2

    Here’s what these buttons do

    Tap ♥ to love a mat and ✕ when it’s not the one. Swiped too fast? ← Back brings it back. My picks show first; tap Tiffany’s picks to see every mat. Tap a color or Holiday to narrow the mats, and tap it again to clear it.

  3. 3

    Your shortlist

    Every mat you ♥ waits at the bottom, so you can compare. When you’re ready, tap Next: add racks.

  4. 4

    Pick 2 racks

    Tap the suitor you love most, then swipe racks the same way, with my rack picks first. Your first two ♥ fill your rack slots; tap × to swap one. Then tap Make it official.

  5. 5

    Make it official

    Pick your view and your size: Story, Post or Pin. Then save your match card or share it with your mahj group.

  6. 6

    Use code MAHJMATCH to save

    Every piece on your card is linked, with each partner brand’s code one tap away.
