Why I love Miss Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Miss Mahjong designs collectible American mahjong sets with hand-painted acrylic tiles, made for players who want their table to reflect their personal style, hence their motto: “Pick your Miss!”

Some of my personal favorites from Miss Mahjong are their Miss Classic tiles in Jade and Malachite, plus their gorgeous Jade & Brass and Opal & Brass 20” acrylic racks, among many others.

Opal racks give you the lightness of white or off-white with a pearly finish that’s softer and, in my opinion, prettier than solid white. After buying my opal racks, I sold my white ones because I simply never reached for them.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
