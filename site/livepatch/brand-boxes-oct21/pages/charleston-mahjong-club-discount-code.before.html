Why I love Charleston Mahjong Club

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Charleston Mahjong Club makes themed sets, and each edition has its own personality, so there’s one for every season, mood and group. Their mascot is the roseate spoonbill, and you’ll spot that Lowcountry charm all through their designs.

Hunt Club is one of my favorite sets for men. Its warm, classic sporting look is perfect for the guys at your table, and it’s a standout in my best mahjong sets for men.

Country Club is a great set for summer by the pool, Après Ski is made for chasing away those winter blues, and who doesn’t love a cocktail? Cocktail Club brings the party. Cabana Club and Yacht Club round out the lineup.

Tip: pick the edition that matches your group’s personality or the season, then add a coordinating neoprene mat so your whole table matches.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
