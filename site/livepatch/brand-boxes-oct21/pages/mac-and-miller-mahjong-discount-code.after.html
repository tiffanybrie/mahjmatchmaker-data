Why I love Mac + Miller Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Mac + Miller’s Heirloom Collection is what put them on my radar. Fire + Fortune comes in two colorways: Verdant Fortune, with rich jade-green marbled backs, and Ember Fire, with deep red tortoise-style backs. Both have beautifully detailed faces with lanterns, bamboo and good-luck knots, and they’re the sets putting this brand on the map.

Their designs are full of New Orleans spirit, from their New Orleans toile to Laissez Les Bons Temps Rouler, a Mardi Gras tile set with fleur-de-lis, beaded borders and French Quarter street lamps, plus a matching purple, gold and green mat. If you’re hunting for Mardi Gras mahjong tiles, this is the set.

The brand was founded by a group of female Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetists (CRNAs). Tip: Heirloom releases come in small batches, so sign up for in-stock notifications if a set you love sells out.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
