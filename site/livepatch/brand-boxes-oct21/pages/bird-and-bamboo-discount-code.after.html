Why I love Bird & Bamboo

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Bird & Bamboo is my go-to for painted wood racks when I want to pull one exact color out of a tile set. With 94 paint colors and stains, it’s the rack brand I use most in my album matches, from Harbor Blue to Jade.

My most-used shades are Weathered Oak, Clear Stain, French Blue and Hot Pink. With so many colors, I built a Bird & Bamboo rack color library to make choosing easier. You’ll see my picks in the rack matches for albums like Willow, Heritage 2.0 and Solara.

Tip: they sell painted wood samples, so you can check a color against your tiles before you order.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
