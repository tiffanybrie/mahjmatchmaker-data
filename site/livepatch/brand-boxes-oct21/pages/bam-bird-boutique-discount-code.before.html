Why I love Bam Bird Boutique

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Bam Bird Boutique makes hand-painted tiles with solid, patterned acrylic backs instead of clear ones, and they come in a wide range of colorways: soft and pretty Classic, muted Origami, vibrant East Meets West, playful MCM, jewel-toned Deco and my favorite, Classic Tortoise.

The price is what sets them apart. Their Origami set in Timber is $295 with a velvet tile bag, which is an incredible value for a hand-painted, solid-backed set. Code MAHJMATCH takes 10% off on top.

Bam Bird Boutique is a Dallas-based mahjong shop founded by neighbors and friends Meredith and Robin. Along with their own tile sets, they carry racks, mats, bags, glassware and even floating game tables for pool days.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
