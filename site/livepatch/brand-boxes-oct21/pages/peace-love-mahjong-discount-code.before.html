Why I love Peace Love Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Peace Love Mahjong makes my favorite budget-friendly wooden racks. Their Oak racks are my most-recommended rack match across the whole site: a natural wood look that goes with nearly every tile set.

They also make modern, affordable tiles and gifts, with playful collections like OG Mahj, Mod and Taylor’s Tiles, Mile High Mahj Club travel sets, and Little Mahj sets made for kids.

Tip: their Collections page pairs tiles, mats and racks into ready-made looks, which is a great shortcut if you’re putting together your first full set.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
