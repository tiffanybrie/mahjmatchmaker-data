About The Mahjong Press

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

The Mahjong Press makes 2026 American mahjong cards, and the fun part is the covers: several are designed to coordinate with specific Miss Mahjong tile sets, so your card can match your table. They also offer a Starter Edition for new players and an Accessible Edition with high contrast and pattern-based suit markers.

If you already play with Miss Mahjong tiles, these are an easy way to finish the look.
