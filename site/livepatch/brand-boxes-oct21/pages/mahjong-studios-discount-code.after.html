About Mahjong Studios

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Mahjong Studios is a newer partner of mine, and here’s what caught my eye: a big range of colorful tile sets and their new Amber Jade tortoise tiles.

Their sets come in bright solids and two-tones (Lemon Lime, Lilac Bloom, Ocean Bloom, Sage Luxe), florals like Peony Garden, and sparkly Glitter Sparkle tiles. They also sell acrylic racks, mats that ship rolled, and tile and rack bags.

They also make handmade tortoise shell acrylic racks with pushers. I haven’t had their racks at my own table yet, so check the details on each listing, like length and finish, before you order.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
