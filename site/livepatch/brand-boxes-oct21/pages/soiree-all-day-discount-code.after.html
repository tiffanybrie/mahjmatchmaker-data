Why I love Soirée All Day

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

As an artist and designer myself, I love finding brands led by creative women who put beautiful art into the world. I’ve admired Hollis’s eye for color in her mahjong paper goods for a long time, so I was thrilled to see her step into the mat game.

My guess? Soirée All Day is just getting started, and I can’t wait to see what she brings to the mahj table next.

Soirée All Day makes the paper side of mahj night: personalized notecards, score card pads, gift tags, art prints and wrapping paper, in prints like Mahjong in Bloom, Cabana Stripe and Chinoiserie. They also make five mahjong mats.

Tip: personalized notecards and score pads make easy hostess gifts, raffle prizes and holiday swap presents.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
