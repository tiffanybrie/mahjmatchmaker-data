Why I love Mahjong Row & Co.

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Mahjong Row & Co. is a brand that feels like it was created out of my own mind. As a personal lover of interior design and the “Studio McGee” aesthetic, these tile sets, mats and racks speak straight into my heart and blend flawlessly into my own home and interior style.

Mahjong Row & Co. was created by founder Tricia, a longtime therapist with a deep love of layered, textured spaces, and it features curated natural, neutral tablescapes that prove texture, pattern and color do not have to be loud in order to make a great impression.

The key to the beautiful tablescapes created through Mahjong Row & Co. tiles, mats and racks is the layering of color, pattern and texture. In my opinion, it’s a contribution to the mahj market that was much needed and is greatly appreciated.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
