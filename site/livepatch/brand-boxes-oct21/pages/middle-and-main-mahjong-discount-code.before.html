Why I love Middle & Main Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Middle & Main Mahjong makes American mahjong sets inspired by Southern places, like their South Carolina, Florida and Georgia sets, plus racks, mats and game-night accessories.

My favorite tortoise racks at the best price point are Middle & Main’s Hawksbill Heritage racks. Many heavy, solid 20” acrylic racks cost far more, and these are every bit as heavy (actually heavier than some) and nearly identical to many top brands. One caveat: the exposure area isn’t fully closed in by a bottom border piece. That doesn’t bother me at all, since I line my exposure tiles up along the top border.

You’ll spot Hawksbill racks all over my Heritage and Tortoise albums, and Barrier Island and Peach & Pine are on my lists of favorite green and peach tiles.

My go-to scalloped wood racks are Middle & Main’s Golden Grain Beechwood racks. They’re solid beechwood, not a wood look, with a scalloped border and a medium natural finish that works with both light and dark tiles. It’s hard to find scalloped wood racks at this price.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
