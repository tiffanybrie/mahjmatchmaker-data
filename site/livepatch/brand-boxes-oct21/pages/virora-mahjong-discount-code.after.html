Why I love Virora Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Virora is where I send friends who want a gorgeous hand-painted set without the designer price tag. Their layered tiles come in the prettiest botanical colorways, and many sets are designed alongside a matching mat, which makes styling a table so easy.

Tip: Virora designs many of its tiles and mats as coordinated pairs, like Coralia with Iriselle and Liliora with Florielle. Buying the pair together is the easiest way to get a matched table in one order.
