Why I love My Fair Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

My Fair Mahjong is known for joyful, colorful, destination-themed mahjong tiles. Their tagline says it all: “Sending you Smiles & Tiles.”

One of my personal favorite things from My Fair Mahjong is their racks! I often recommend their clear 20” acrylic racks as a first rack. They’re high-quality, heavy acrylic at a great price, and clear matches any and every tile and mat. They come in a rainbow of other colors too, but I really love the option to buy just their wavy pushers and add them to your clear racks.

A great starting combination is their clear racks plus the wavy tortoise pushers. Now you have two high-quality, solid 20” acrylic rack looks for about the cost of a single set from many other brands.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
