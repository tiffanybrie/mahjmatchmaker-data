Why I love Mahj Mood

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Mahj Mood’s Radiant Mood Collection has two of my picks. Their White Pearl tiles are a single layer of white pearl acrylic with soft pink, teal and green faces, and they’re in my most versatile mahjong tiles because the near-white backs go with almost any mat. Their Mauve-Blush tiles, with cat-eye mauve backs that shift in the light, are on my list of favorite pink mahjong tiles. And their new Eloise set, with blonde tortoise-inspired backs, is a pretty neutral that reminds me of Talulah.

They also sell the rack setup I recommend most: clear scalloped acrylic racks with tortoise pusher add-ons. Clear goes with every tile set, and the tortoise pushers add warmth. You can swap in other pusher colors, like white pearl, pink or mint, to refresh racks you already own.

Mahj Mood was started by two moms bringing fresh energy to a classic game. Many of their tile sets sell on pre-order and their racks and pushers sell out, so sign up for restock alerts on anything you love.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
