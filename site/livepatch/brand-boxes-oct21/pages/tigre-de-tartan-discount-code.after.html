Why I love Tigre de Tartán

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Tigre de Tartán mats are artist-designed and made in small batches, and they appear in almost every album I’ve made. Their mats are the star of the show. Unlike most mahjong mats, they aren’t neoprene: they’re a vegan leatherette, with a smooth, luxe finish. Some even have the rules printed right on them, which is handy for newer players.

Tigre de Tartán doesn’t have a huge selection of rack colors, but I really love the ones they do have. Their Rich Raisin racks (which almost read as a wood tone) and Marine Blue beechwood racks are great moody colors that are surprisingly versatile across a lot of moody tablescapes. I also love their scalloped blonde tortoise racks, a light, beige-and-brown tortoise.

One thing to know: Tigre de Tartán racks have one of the shallower viewing pitches, so tiles stand more upright. I still find the angle OK, and I use these racks regularly. Read more in My Favorite Mahjong Racks.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
