Why I love Splash of Color

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Splash of Color has one of the biggest mat selections out there: chinoiserie, florals, holiday prints, city and travel themes, and a whole William Morris-inspired botanical line, plus matching tiles, racks and shufflers.

Their Asian Chinoiserie mat is a showstopper with my Amara album. It has the same super wide color range as the Chinoiserie Songbird, so bright and pastel sets both find a match.

Their Hydrangea Blooms mat is one I use a lot, with Jade, Malachite and Lagoon, and the Preppy Pastel Animal Print mat is a fun match for Glow Wild.

Tip: with this many prints, search their site by your tile colors. Their mats come in a lot of shapes too, including round and scalloped.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
