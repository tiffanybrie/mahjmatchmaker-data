Why I love Mahj Squad

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Mahj Squad designs themed collections where the tiles, mat, tote and tile bag all coordinate. Their tile sets are hand painted, with custom ink colors filled into each engraving.

Palm Springs is the one I come back to: vibrant pink tile tops, palm trees, flamingos and sunny yellow accents, inspired by 1960s Palm Springs. It made my favorite pink mahjong tiles list, and the matching Palm Springs mat shows up in my Amara and Glow Wild albums.

Beyond Palm Springs, their mats lean preppy and pretty: chinoiserie in pink, blue, jade and pastel, plus Christmas mats like Clara’s Dream and Blue Christmas that I included in my Christmas guide.

Tip: mats, tablecloths and totes come in the same prints, so you can match your whole table in one order and use MAHJMATCH once.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
