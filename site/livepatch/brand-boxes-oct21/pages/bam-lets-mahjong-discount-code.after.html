What caught my eye at Bam! Let's Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Bam! Let’s Mahjong makes small-batch tile sets that are deeply engraved and hand painted, each with a strong theme and a lot of personality.

The Palm Royale set is the one that caught my eye first: seashell dots, palm tree bams, coral craks in pink and tangerine, and seahorse dragons. They also make Nantucket, a nautical set with sailboat bams, the Southern-inspired Mint and Roses and Grandmillennial sets, a bold Art Deco set, and a Holly Jolly Christmas set.

They round out the table with mats in matching themes, acrylic racks with scallop pushers in clear, white and black, travel sets, and even fabric-wrapped mahjong coffee table books.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
