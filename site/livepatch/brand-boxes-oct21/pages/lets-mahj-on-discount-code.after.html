What caught my eye at Let's Mahj On

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Let’s Mahj On is a one-stop shop for styling the whole table: themed tile sets, mats, scalloped tablecloths, acrylic racks, cups, napkins and even cordless table lamps.

Their tile sets each tell a story: Let’s Get Nautical! with sailboats, starfish and sea creatures, Let’s Garden Party! with soft florals and hummingbirds, a tennis set, a tropical set, and the Let’s Be Iconic! sets in rich jewel tones. Let’s Be Iconic! Jeweled Green made my favorite green tiles list, and Let’s Get Nautical! is on my favorite blue tiles list.

Where they really stand out is the table itself. Their scalloped tablecloths in prints like Blue Pagoda and Pink Botanical Stripe are a pretty way to dress up a card table, and their clear and pink scalloped acrylic racks fit right into a styled game night.

Tip: if you host, a tablecloth plus a coordinating mat changes the whole look of your table for less than a new tile set.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
