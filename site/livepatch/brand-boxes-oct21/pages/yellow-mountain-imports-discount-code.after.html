Why I recommend Yellow Mountain Imports

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Yellow Mountain Imports is my go-to suggestion when someone wants a pretty set at a friendlier price. Many of their American sets come complete with racks, pushers and a case.

Their colors hold their own: Verdant Spring and Jade Horizon are on my favorite green tiles list as some of the most affordable green sets, and Harbor Tide, with cobalt blue backs, is on my favorite blue tiles list.

If you’re a traditionalist, The Classic is a great place to start: ivory tiles with engraved markings in the traditional colors. I talk about why in tile set details to know.
