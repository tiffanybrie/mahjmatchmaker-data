Why I love Bespoke Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Bespoke makes statement pieces: bold tiles, artist collaborations and the Year of the Dragon mats that show up all over my albums.

Some of my favorite items from Bespoke are their entire Year of the Dragon collection, truly some of the most versatile mats on the market! I particularly love the periwinkle colorway, but you’ll see all the colorways sprinkled throughout my tile matching albums.

I also really love their Highlighter Pink racks. Their pictures don’t do them justice. They bring pink to the table without committing to a solid tone, and they work across lots of different pinks. Bespoke racks are very heavy acrylic, which is reflected in their price, but they are quite special and a great splurge for your mahj collection.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
