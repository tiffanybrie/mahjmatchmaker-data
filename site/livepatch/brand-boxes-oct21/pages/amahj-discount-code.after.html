Why I like Amahj for new players

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Amahj’s big difference is that every tile is clearly labeled. That makes their sets a great choice for teaching, for new players, and for anyone who wants a quicker read on their hand.

Their Signature sets are hand engraved and hand painted, in colors like White, Light Blue and Lilac. The Light Blue Signature is on my favorite blue tiles list.

For beginners, kids and families, the stamped Foundation set is a simpler, friendlier-priced option, and their squiggle game mats make the tiles pop.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
