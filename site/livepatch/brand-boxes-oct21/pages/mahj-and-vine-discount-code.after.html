Why I love Mahj & Vine

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Mahj & Vine makes mats with a lot of color in them: chinoiserie, watercolor florals and botanicals in pinks, greens, blues and oranges. With that many colors, most tile sets find something to pick up.

The Chinoiserie Songbird is my favorite mat for pink tiles, hands down. Every friend I have who loves pink absolutely dies over this one. It’s my top match for Amara, and it shows up in my Dandy and Glow Wild albums and my most versatile mats list.

Their mats appear across my albums with Solara, Jade and Lagoon too, from Greenly and Zesty Plume to Watercolor Bloom.

Tip: if you play with more than one tile set, pick a mat with lots of colors, like Songbird or Watercolor Bloom. It will work with nearly everything.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
