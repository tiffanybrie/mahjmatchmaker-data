Why I love Hip Hip Mahjong

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Hip Hip Mahjong builds celebration-themed collections, with tiles, mats, racks and shufflers that coordinate for each holiday.

My favorite is their holiday Rackin’ Rudolph collection: red-backed tiles, a fawn-print mat framed in tartan and red bows, and matching shufflers. Their I ❤️ Mahj tiles and mat are just as cute around Valentine’s Day. The Rudolph tiles and mat sell out fast, and the new release launches October 8, so grab them early for Christmas game nights.

Their collections cover holidays and special occasions, from Mahj-O-Ween and the Hoppy EAST-Hare Collection to Happy Birthday and Hey Baby, plus racks with names like Trophy Wife and Trust Fund.

Tip: since tiles, mats and shufflers come as matching collections, bundle the pieces you want in one order so MAHJMATCH covers everything.
