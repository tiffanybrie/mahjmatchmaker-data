Why I love Mini Mahjer

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Mahjong instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Kids love mahjong for the same reasons we do: the clack of the tiles, the pretty sets, and the moment a hand comes together. The NMJL card can overwhelm them at first, though, and full-size sets are heavy for little hands.

That’s where Mini Mahjer comes in. You don’t even need a new set to get kids playing: start with a few Mini Mahjer Cards and use the tiles you already own. My favorite part is that grown-ups can play their regular NMJL card at the same table while the kids play theirs, so everybody plays together.

Mini Mahjer was also one of Oprah’s Favorite Things in 2025 and is a Mom’s Choice Award recipient.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker
