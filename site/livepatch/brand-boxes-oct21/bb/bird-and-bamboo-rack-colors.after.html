Bird & Bamboo rack FAQ

How many colors do Bird & Bamboo racks come in?

Their 20" wooden rack and pusher set comes in dozens of paint colors, including metallics.

How much are Bird & Bamboo racks?

The 20" set of 4 racks and pushers with a canvas rack bag is $135 with standard pushers or $150 with scalloped pushers. Code MAHJMATCH takes 10% off.

How do I pick a rack color?

Start with one color you love in your tiles, then find it here or in the rack matches on my tile match albums. A neutral like White, Cream or True Black is the safest choice if you play with more than one set.

Can I see a color in person first?

Yes. Bird & Bamboo sells painted wood samples for $6, so you can hold a color up to your tiles before ordering.

How long do Bird & Bamboo racks take to ship?

Each set is made to order and ships in 7 to 9 business days. Rush production may be available for an extra fee.

Will Bird & Bamboo racks fit my tiles?

The racks are 20" long with a 1.57" tile exposure, and they fit Oh My Mahjong, The Mahjong Line and most other tile brands.
