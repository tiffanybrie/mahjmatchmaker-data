Find Your Mat-ch

Who picks the matches?
Tiffany’s picks are built right in. While the button says Tiffany’s picks: on, every mat you see is her own match for your set. Tap it to swipe the whole library and make your own matches.

I already have a mat. Can I start there?
Yes. Tap Start with a mat and swipe through tile sets that match it, then add racks.

How do I save?
Use code MAHJMATCH to save at 30+ partner brands. Your match card links every piece, and each brand’s code page has the details.

Does it cost anything?
No. Find Your Mat-ch is free, with nothing to sign up for. Swipe as much as you like.

Mats

What’s the difference between Dapper 1 and Dapper 2?
Oh My Mahjong has made two versions of its Dapper mat, and both are now retired. Dapper 1 is the original, a browner, olive-toned plaid; Dapper 2 is the newer version, a more burgundy plaid. When you’re hunting for a retired Dapper, check which one you mean: the brown one or the burgundy one.
