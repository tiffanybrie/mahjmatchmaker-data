Here's what partnering with Mahj Matchmaker looks like.

Your own partner brand page

Every partner brand gets its own page on mahjmatchmaker.com, highlighting my favorite products and picks from your line, plus every place players can find your products across the site: tile set albums, matches and blog articles. Your code (MAHJMATCH) is front and center, and every product links straight to your shop, so it's easy for players to go from "oh, I love that" to checkout.

Find Your Match, launching October 27

Find Your Match is our new swipe-to-match tool. Players start with the tiles they own or the mat they love, swipe through the mats and racks I'd pair with them, and save a match card to share with their mahj group.

There's nothing you need to upload. If your shop runs on Shopify, I pull your catalog in automatically, and your products are set up for desktop, mobile and the shareable match cards. With a very large line, I curate my favorites so the matching stays focused and every product gets a fair shot.

FAQ

Do you offer paid placements?

No. I don't offer any paid placements on the site.

Do you accept product samples?

Yes! Many brands send samples for color matching. Sample sets or swatches are very helpful for getting the best color match, but they aren't required for a partnership. If you have a new product launching soon, reach out with the details and we can talk about it.

Does every partner brand get a match album?

Partner brands are given preference for a match album for at least one tile set in their line. An album isn't guaranteed and often won't be immediate. Which sets get albums is always at my discretion: I weigh market demand, uniqueness, artistic quality and seasonality, plus my own taste. Our readers send album requests all the time, and I keep track of the most-requested sets and weigh that heavily. If there's a set from your line you'd love to see in an album, tell me and I'll do my best to honor it. And yes, a brand can absolutely get more than one album!

Will you review my products on your blog?

A first-hand review means I've held it, used it or played with it, so I only write those for products I have in hand. Products can still be recommended on the site for other reasons, like how well they match. Sending a sample doesn't guarantee a feature or a positive review: you'll always get my honest opinion.

How do players find my products?

Through your partner brand page, the Discount Codes page, my tile set albums, Find Your Match, blog articles and our Facebook group, where we share matches and real-life tables.
