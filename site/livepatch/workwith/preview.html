To shoppers, ShopMy brands and brands with no affiliation look the same: no discount. The ♥ and the code are what set partners apart, and they're the easiest way to turn a match into a sale.

Why affiliate programs make sense (especially for small brands)

You pay only when you get paid

Commission comes out of a sale that already happened. No sale, no cost.

Zero wasted ad spend

Ads charge for views and clicks whether anyone buys. An affiliate program never does.

About a 10x return on ad spend

At a 10% commission, every $10 you pay out arrived with a $100 order. That's roughly a 10x return, every time.

Reach shoppers who won't pay full price

Some shoppers wait for a code before they buy. A code gives them a reason to buy from you.

Build it into your margins

Like "free shipping," the cost lives in your pricing. There's no such thing as free marketing, and this is the kind that only costs you when it works.

FAQ

Do you offer paid placements?

No. I don't offer any paid placements on the site.

Will my products appear if I don't have an affiliate program?

They can. Find Your Mat-ch always shows the best color matches, so products I've matched or picked can appear from any brand. Without a code, they're listed below the partner brands and marked "no discount available." An affiliate program with code MAHJMATCH moves you up into the partner group.

Does every partner brand get a match album?

If your brand sells tile sets, yes. I commit to producing at least one match album for every partner brand that sells tile sets. Album timing is subject to my content plan, so it often won't be immediate. Please share your desired tile set if you have a preference.

Beyond that, which sets get albums is my call: I weigh market demand, uniqueness, artistic quality and seasonality, plus my own taste. Our readers send album requests all the time, and I keep track of the most-requested sets and weigh that heavily. And yes, a brand can absolutely get more than one album!

My shop doesn't run on Shopify. Can I still be in Find Your Mat-ch?

Yes! If your shop runs on Shopify, I pull your catalog in automatically. If it runs on something else, I add your products by hand from your shop. Either way, there's nothing you need to upload.

Will you review my products on your blog?

A first-hand review means I've held it, used it or played with it, so I only write those for products I have in hand. Products can still be recommended on the site for other reasons, like how well they match. My reviews are always honest, but never harsh. I share what I love about a product, plus the helpful details shoppers need to know (like what size tiles a rack fits), so they can choose what's right for them.

When I really love something, I give it a badge on the site, like "Tiffany's Favorite," Best Value, Most Unique or Rare Edition. Just as "Tiffany's Matches" gives shoppers a confidence boost in Find Your Mat-ch, badges are a friendly guidepost through the many products on the market. They're reserved for things I've personally used and truly love.

But badges aren't the final word. The north star of Mahj Matchmaker is helping shoppers find their perfect set and accessories, not copy mine. So I leave plenty of room around my favorites to share what I genuinely like about other products too. Something that isn't on my own table might be exactly right for someone else's.

Do you accept product samples or gifting?

Yes, we do. Many brands send samples for color matching. Sample sets or swatches are very helpful for getting the best color match, but they aren't required for a partnership. If you have a new product launching soon, or simply want your products considered for personal recommendations and editorial content, feel free to send. I will always give my honest opinion. When I have a product in hand, I'm also happy to photograph it for Find Your Mat-ch if your brand's default product photography style or angle isn't ideal for the app.

How do players find my products?

Through your partner brand page, my tile set albums, the Find Your Mat-ch tool, blog articles and styled table mock-ups, social media and our Facebook group, where we share matches, real-life tables, and upcoming launches, restocks and release dates.

I have an upcoming launch or restock. Can I tell you about it?

Yes, please do! Content planning is constant for us, so the earlier you can let us know about your upcoming launches, re-launches and restocks, the better. This form lands right in my social media calendar and is the best way to tell us what you have coming up.

We also subscribe to all of our partner brands' newsletters and regularly plan content around them. If you don't do a newsletter, the form is a great way to share your plans so we can help promote them. We understand some brands prefer to announce on social media to their entire audience, affiliates included. We'll do our best to keep up with those posts, but they're the least reliable way to get into our content.
