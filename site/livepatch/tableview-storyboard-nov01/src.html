Find Your Mat-ch™ · storyboard for review

Table view, step by step

How setting the table would work inside the app, phone first. Every mat, tile and rack picture here is a real asset: Papaya Spritz tiles on the Middle & Main Fruit Stand vendor photo with Emily's Bird & Bamboo racks (Canva p.51, left lilac), plus Nature Walk on Sunday Rattan. Tap any screen to see it bigger.

  • Mat = vendor photo, never AI
  • Tile backs drawn from real size
  • No pusher for now
  • Walls on / off
  • Up to 4 racks
  • Full table only with a top-down photo that passes
  • Corner works for any mat
Your call: reply with what to change, or OK and we build a working sample with a couple of rack colors.
Screen 1 · where it lives

A third option by the deck

Table sits next to Up close and Aerial in the View switch you already moved down by the mat. Nothing new to hunt for.

Screen 2 · open it

The table, already set

It opens on the full table with 4 racks and walls built, tile photo in the same round inset as Aerial. One row of controls; the rest waits under Adjust.

Screen 3 · adjust

Every control in one sheet

Adjust slides up over the bottom of the table and you can still see it change behind. Swipe down or tap the table to close.

Screen 4 · walls off

Walls on or off

Walls off is just mat and racks, for judging the mat-to-rack pairing. Your choices stay as chips under the table.

Screen 5 · get close

Zoom into one wall

Pinch to see the tile backs right against the rack and the mat behind them. The zoom holds while you change settings.

Screen 6 · corner view

Corner, your seat at the table

Two walls and their racks, like looking down at your own spot. Flip switches bottom + right to bottom + left.

Screen 7 · rack color

Try another rack color

Tap a swatch and the racks change in place. The sample stays at two colors; more get added once the look is right.

Screen 8 · swap

Swap the tiles or the mat

Your matches for this mat come first, then everything else. Swap mat only lists mats with a passing top-down photo for Full table.

Screen 9 · no top-down photo

When a mat has no top-down photo

Table view still opens, straight into Corner, with Full table greyed out and one plain line saying why. (Pictured with Nature Walk on Sunday Rattan; Rattan does have a top-down photo.)

Screen 10 · finish

Save, share, shop the look

Save keeps this table on their match card (rack color and view included). Share sends the picture with a link back. Shop lists tiles, mat and racks.

Screen 11 · desktop

On a computer: table left, controls right

Same controls, no sliding sheet. The Adjust panel is always open beside the table, and Shop the look sits at the bottom of it.

Open points for the working sample (not decided here): which rack the shop link points to for each color; whether Racks 1–3 can be offered on mats whose own photo shows props at the edges (Fruit Stand's vendor photo has a clear rack on its sides, so on that mat fewer than 4 racks would show it); and which partners we ask for top-down photos first.
