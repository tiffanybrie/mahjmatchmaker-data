Retro Rattan Holiday: a red tartan, a ceramic tree lamp and the Sunday Mahjong Rattan mat. No Christmas mat or tiles required.

This one makes me happy. It's a little bit retro, a little bit grandma's house at Christmas, and it uses a mat that plays all year.

Same idea as my Polo Club holiday table: you don't need a Christmas mat. Pick a mat with warm colors, add a tablecloth and one fun centerpiece, and let the styling do the work.

The mat: Sunday Mahjong Rattan

Sunday Mahjong's Rattan mat has an olive green field with a terracotta cane weave and a chocolate brown frame. Those warm, earthy tones are what make it work for a retro Christmas.

The tiles: Oh My Mahjong Heritage

Heritage again, because it's that versatile. The traditional red, green and blue faces and the warm tortoise backs look right at home here.

The racks: walnut

Bam Bird Boutique Walnut racks are warm and rich against the rattan. If you'd rather go lighter, Peace Love Mahjong Oak works too.

Setting the table

  • Tablecloth: a red tartan 60x60 square tablecloth. The red picks up the terracotta in the weave, and the green ties back to the mat. I go 60x60 on my 38.5" card table for a little more drape.
  • The centerpiece: a ceramic Christmas tree lamp. It's a new piece with that retro ceramic tree look, and it's the only tall thing on the table, so the middle stays open.
  • Coasters: amber ribbed glass coasters. The amber glass glows with the warm browns in the mat.
  • Dice dish: a holly leaf ceramic dish. Green with red berries and just the right size for the dice.

That's it. Keep it simple so there's still room for tiles.

Retro holiday mahjong table FAQ

Can I make a retro Christmas mahjong table without Christmas tiles?

Yes. Start with a mat that already has red, green or warm earthy tones, like Sunday Mahjong's Rattan, and neutral tiles like Oh My Mahjong Heritage. Add a red tartan tablecloth and a ceramic tree lamp, and the table reads retro Christmas without a single holiday tile.

What tablecloth goes with the Sunday Mahjong Rattan mat for the holidays?

A red tartan. It picks up the warm terracotta in the rattan weave and the green in the mat's field. I use a 60x60 square on my 38.5" card table so it has a little drape.

What racks go with Oh My Mahjong Heritage on the Rattan mat?

Warm wood. Bam Bird Boutique Walnut is my pick here. Peace Love Mahjong Oak and Middle & Main Hawksbill Heritage Tortoise work too.

How do I keep a holiday mahjong table from getting cluttered?

Pick one centerpiece and a couple of small pieces. On this table that's the ceramic tree lamp, a holly leaf dish for the dice and one coaster per player. Leave the rest of the table open for tiles.
