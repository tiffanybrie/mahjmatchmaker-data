No Christmas mat, no Christmas tiles, no problem. Here's how I style a holiday mahjong table with the tiles and mat you already own.

Here's a secret: you don't need a Christmas mat to have a Christmas mahjong table. Last year I played Oh My Mahjong Heritage on Oh My Mahjong's Dapper mat with walnut racks. I added a little burlap-wrapped evergreen, some brass coasters and a brass lamp that felt a little dressed up. It looked great, and I didn't buy a single holiday tile.

The trick is styling. Bring the Christmas in with what's around the mat, and pull your colors from things you already have.

The mat: Luxe Mahjong The Polo Club

This year I'd style it on Luxe Mahjong's The Polo Club mat. It's a green and navy tartan border with thin gold and red lines around a deep navy center. It reads holiday without a single ornament on it, and it plays all year.

The tiles: Oh My Mahjong Heritage

Heritage is a neutral set with traditional red, green and blue faces and warm tortoise backs. That's why it's such a great holiday set: the colors are already there. This is why you buy versatile tiles. Come holiday time, all you need is the styling.

Setting the table

  • Tablecloth: a hunter green square tablecloth picks up the green in the tartan and lets the mat stand out.
  • Greenery: little burlap-wrapped trees. They come in a set of four, so tuck one by the lamp and save the rest for the bar cart.
  • Dice dish: a solid brass clawfoot dish gives the dice a home and adds a little sparkle.
  • Coasters: hammered brass coasters with velvet backs, so they won't scratch the table.
  • Lighting: a gold cordless table lamp for a warm glow with no cord across the table.

Holiday mahjong table FAQ

Do I need a Christmas mat for a holiday mahjong table?

No. A mat with a red, green or plaid colorway, like Luxe Mahjong's Polo Club or Oh My Mahjong's Dapper, can carry a holiday table. Add a little greenery, a tablecloth and some brass, and it feels like Christmas without buying a themed mat or tiles.

What mahjong tiles look good for the holidays?

Neutral tiles with traditional red, green and blue faces, like Oh My Mahjong Heritage, look right at home on a holiday table. They play year round, so come December all you need is the styling.

Which mats work for Christmas and year round?

Mats that are mostly green, red or plaid pull double duty. A few I love: Luxe Mahjong The Polo Club, Oh My Mahjong Dapper, Oh My Mahjong Liberty and Sunday Mahjong Rattan.

How do you decorate a mahjong table for Christmas?

Pull colors from your mat. Lay down a tablecloth in one of its shades, add a small evergreen, a dice dish and a lamp, and keep the center clear for play.
