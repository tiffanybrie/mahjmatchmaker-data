The Rackin' Rudolph mat is one of my favorite Christmas mats. A plaid border with bows on the corners, and a reindeer doe print in the middle that makes it a really unique holiday mat. So I styled the whole table around it.

The mat and tiles: Hip Hip Mahjong Rackin' Rudolph

The Rackin' Rudolph mat and the Rackin' Rudolph tiles are made to go together. The tiles have reindeer, Santa and ornament faces, and their red backs match the bows on the mat.

The racks: Middle & Main scalloped Milk Glass

Middle & Main's Milk Glass racks are my favorite holiday racks. Solid white acrylic with a scalloped edge. They pick up the white border on this mat and really stand out against the dark tartan, and they play year round.

Setting the table

  • Tablecloth: a cherry red 60x60 square tablecloth. It matches the bright red bows. I go 60x60 on my 38.5" card table for a little drape.
  • The centerpiece: a gold cordless bow lamp. The bow on the lamp echoes the bows on the mat's corners, and with no cord it doesn't get in the way.
  • Dice dish: an enamel reindeer box. It's a little trinket box with a lid, and the dice go inside. Too cute.
  • Coasters: white marble coasters. Clean and simple next to the white racks.

Rackin' Rudolph table FAQ

What racks go with the Rackin' Rudolph mat?

Middle & Main's scalloped Milk Glass racks. The solid white picks up the mat's white border and stands out against the dark tartan, and the scallop keeps it fun.

What tablecloth goes with Rackin' Rudolph?

A cherry red. The bows on the mat's corners are a bright cherry red, so a solid red cloth ties right in. I use a 60x60 square on my 38.5" card table.

Do the Rackin' Rudolph tiles match the mat?

Yes. Hip Hip Mahjong made them as a pair, with reindeer, Santa and ornament faces and red backs that match the bows.

What's a fun dice dish for a Christmas mahjong table?

A little trinket box works great. I use an enamel reindeer box with a lid and keep the dice inside.
