Festive racks

Honestly? You don't need to buy holiday racks. If you already own red or green racks, pull them out. They make a great holiday add-on. My personal opinion is that you should get racks you can play with year round. My favorite holiday rack is one of those: Middle & Main Mahjong's Milk Glass racks, solid white acrylic with a scalloped edge. They work all year, and they're so fun to pull out at the holidays.

Scalloped, white, clear and pearlescent racks are neutral enough for any season, and they still bring a little playfulness and fun to a holiday table. My Fair Mahjong's Pearl White racks have that shimmer. If you'd like to really get themed out, holiday racks are an option too. Hip Hip Mahjong's Santa Baby racks are one example.

And as always, clear racks let the mat and tiles shine. They're always my recommendation for your very first set of racks. See more ideas in My Favorite Mahjong Racks.

How to style a Christmas mahjong table

  • Match your mahjong table to your holiday style. Are you a more-is-more person, or do you keep things simple? If your holiday decor leans sophisticated or traditional, go for a tartan plaid or a traditional pattern mat. If you decorate on the brighter side, look at pastel and bright mats. They'll fit right in with the rest of your holiday decor.
  • Go beyond red and green. Don't count out tile colors that aren't traditional holiday colors. Oh My Mahjong Gatsby is a deep teal navy set, and it looks great on the Oh My Mahjong Winterberry mat. Love pink tiles? Oh My Mahjong Amara is so pretty on the Oh My Mahjong Clara mat, with its hot pink border. Simply pick a mat that has your tile color in it. Shop the mats that match your tiles.
  • Own neutral tiles? You only need a holiday mat. A neutral set like Oh My Mahjong Heritage looks beautiful on a holiday mat, especially since its faces are traditional red, green and blue. This is why you buy versatile tiles: come holiday time, all you need is a mat. See My Most Versatile Mahjong Tile Sets.

Christmas Mahjong FAQ

Are there Christmas mahjong tile sets?

Yes. Anders Ruff makes the new Dear Santa set, Mahj Squad makes Nutcracker Ballet, Virora Mahjong makes Tinselle and Nivalee, Bam! Let's Mahjong makes Holly Jolly in Rudolph Red, Grinch Green and Sugarplum Pink, Hip Hip Mahjong makes Rackin' Rudolph, Charleston Mahjong Club makes the winter Après Ski Club set, and Peace Love Mahjong makes Spirit of Christmas. I've matched them with mats and racks on my holiday matches page.

What is a good Christmas mahjong mat?

This isn't a one-size-fits-all answer. The best Christmas mahjong mat is the one that fits your style and your budget. If you'd rather not buy a themed tile set, you're looking for the Christmas mat that suits the tiles you already own. Start with my list of the best Christmas mats for the tiles you already own. If you have a Christmas tile set, there are tons of matches to choose from on my holiday matches page. Or browse my gallery of Christmas mahjong mats and see what speaks to you.

Can I build a holiday look without buying Christmas tiles or a Christmas mat?

Yes! Mats with a mostly green or red colorway, or a plaid pattern, make great holiday mats, and they play year round too. The ones that come to mind are Sunday Mahjong Rattan, Oh My Mahjong Liberty, Luxe Mahjong's Polo Club and Oh My Mahjong Dapper. Pair one with neutral tiles or a set you already own, then bring in the Christmas with your styling: a tablecloth, a centerpiece of holiday greenery, a fun lamp or a dice dish. Pull colors from things you already have. That's what I did last year: I played Oh My Mahjong Heritage on the Dapper mat with walnut racks, a little burlap-wrapped evergreen tree, brass coasters and a brass lamp that felt a little dressed up for the holidays. It looked great.
