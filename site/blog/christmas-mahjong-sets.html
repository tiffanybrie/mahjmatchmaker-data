Festive racks

Red racks are an instant holiday upgrade. Bespoke Mahjong's Ultimate Rack in Holiday Red is a splurge-worthy pick, and clear acrylic racks always work if you'd rather let the mat be the star. See more ideas in My Favorite Mahjong Racks.

How to style a Christmas mahjong table

  • Pick one star. If your mat is busy and festive, choose calmer tiles, or let a holiday tile set shine on a simpler mat.
  • Go beyond red and green. Blue, white and pearly tones make a soft, wintry Christmas table.
  • Use what you own. A neutral set like Oh My Mahjong Heritage or a tortoise set looks beautiful on a holiday mat. See My Most Versatile Mahjong Tile Sets.

Christmas Mahjong FAQ

Are there Christmas mahjong tile sets?

Yes. Anders Ruff makes the new Dear Santa set, Mahj Squad makes Nutcracker Ballet, Virora Mahjong makes Tinselle and Nivalee, Bam! Let's Mahjong makes Holly Jolly in Rudolph Red, Grinch Green and Sugarplum Pink, Hip Hip Mahjong makes Rackin' Rudolph, Charleston Mahjong Club makes the winter Après Ski Club set, and Peace Love Mahjong makes Spirit of Christmas. I've matched them with mats and racks on my holiday matches page.

What is a good Christmas mahjong mat?

Oh My Mahjong has several holiday mats, including Holly Jolly, Winterberry, Yuletide, Merriment, Clara and Sugarplum. Peace Love Mahjong has thirteen, including Vintage Noel, Nutcracker Noel and Poinsettia & Peace. Virora Hollivia, Mahj Squad Clara's Dream and Blue Christmas, Bam Bird Boutique's Holiday mat and Bespoke Mahjong's Holiday Love mat and Bird & Bamboo's The Ornamental are great options too.
