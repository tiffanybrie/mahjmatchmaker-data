How to style a Christmas mahjong table

  • Pick one star. If your mat is busy and festive, choose calmer tiles, or let a holiday tile set shine on a simpler mat.
  • Go beyond red and green. Blue, white and pearly tones make a soft, wintry Christmas table.
  • Use what you own. A neutral set like Oh My Mahjong Heritage or a tortoise set looks beautiful on a holiday mat. See My Most Versatile Mahjong Tile Sets.
