Slide 2 · Q1: Is it free? Do I have to download anything?
A: Free, and nothing to download. It works right in your browser at mahjmatchmaker.com/find-your-match. Tip: add it to your home screen so it's one tap away.

Slide 3 · Q2: What's the difference between a partner brand and a non-partner brand?
A: Partner brands are brands I'm affiliated with. I earn a small commission on anything you buy there with code MAHJMATCH, and you get a discount, usually 10% or more. Non-partner brands simply aren't affiliates, but they're still listed. We separate them so you know where your code works. We put partners first, and we'll keep bringing in non-partner brands and cleaning up their catalogs.

Slide 4 · Q3: How do I get the discount?
A: Use code MAHJMATCH at checkout on a partner brand's site. In Shop by brand, partners are listed first and labeled. If a brand has its own terms (like a minimum order), it's noted right there.

Slide 5 · Q4: How did you match these?
A: By eye, by color. This all started with mockups I made for my own table. Turn on Tiffany's picks to see only mine. Everything else is sorted by color, so you can find your own favorites too.

Slide 6 · Q5: Can I start with a mat I already own?
A: Yes! Tap Start with a mat, pick yours, and swipe tile sets on it. ♥ the ones you love, ✕ the rest, then add racks.

Slide 7 · Q6: Does it show holiday mats?
A: Yes, holiday is here! Tap Holiday for Christmas, Hanukkah and snowy winter tile sets and mats, then add racks and see your whole table together. My holiday albums are on the site too.

Slide 8 · Q7: Can I save the ones I love?
A: Yes! Tap the star on any tile set, mat or rack and find it in My stars, top right. Right now they save on the device you're using. Sign-in is coming, so your stars can follow you everywhere.

Slide 9 · Q8: Why don't I see my favorite brand?
A: We're still bringing in more brands, partner and non-partner. Want one we don't have yet? Scroll to the bottom of the tool and tap Tell me here. It's your tool!
