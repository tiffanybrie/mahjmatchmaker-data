## Brand reshare strategy
Brands repost content that makes THEIR product look amazing and sends sales to them. So each reel:
1. makes one partner's holiday line the star, with real brand product photos and nothing pasted onto a mat. The tiles always sit in their own framed inset (mat full, tiles inset, the match-card style).
2. uses what the brand is pushing right now, from its last ~12 Instagram posts (Planoly, Nov 1, 12:10am). Anders Ruff: Dear Santa shipping, Christmas preorders, #ChristmasMahjong. Virora: Tinselle Emerald Green is back in stock, and the Christmas Collection. Bam! Let's Mahjong: the "Fridays until Christmas" countdown, pink, bows and gift tone.
3. shows how deep that line goes in our albums and in Find Your Mat-ch, with real counts. Then it shows the tool doing the matching, because partners still don't understand what we do (12:22am). The match card appears only as the OUTPUT of that flow.
4. goes out as an Instagram collab invite to the brand once Tiffany approves. A collab puts the reel on their grid too, and that is the reshare.

## QA notes
- center_check: every designed frame passes with the sparkle/snow layer off (`NO_FX=1`), except two deliberate exceptions. The first is the tile inset that sits bottom-right on purpose (the match-card style). The second is the Bam end-card colorway labels, which differ in width under centred tiles. With the effects on, the checker counts sparkles as content and flags them.
- FYM-REEL-RULES: ✕ and ♥ appear in both steps. The camera is fixed on every swipe: only the card flies, with a stamp, and the next real card shows underneath. The camera moves only between actions. The tiles strip and mat show together on every mat swipe. There is a + + zoom in the racks step. Every brand on screen is named (stickers) and tagged.
- Safe zones: key text sits between y 250 and 1520. The app's own bottom bar can fall below 1520, but our name stickers repeat what it says.
- Rendering: `src/` (PIL at 30 fps, frames in /dev/shm, deleted after encode). The real app screens come from `src/cap_fym.py`, which loads the live site's FYM HTML (Version 1.10) locally in headless Chromium at 390×844 @3x. Configs are in `src/cfg/`, and capture meta is in `src/cap/`. Product photos come from each brand's Shopify product JSON (`src/fetch_photos.py`).
