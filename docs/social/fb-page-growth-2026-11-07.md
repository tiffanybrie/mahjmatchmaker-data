# Facebook PAGE growth (21 followers) + can the group be revived? (research Nov 7 2026, DRAFT)

## Does Facebook suggest Pages the way it suggests groups?
Yes. Meta runs a "Pages You May Like" recommendation system (it has its own Meta transparency explainer), and Facebook has long shown similar-Page suggestions after someone follows a Page. Meta doesn't publish the exact signals; in practice suggestions follow engagement (people who react to, comment on or share a Page's posts, and people similar to its followers). A Page with almost no engagement is rarely suggested, so the first followers have to come from our own audiences.

## Can a stalled Facebook group be revived?
What groups are ranked on now (Lilach Bullock, updated Oct 20 2026; partly her own data): conversation between members (comments and replies), native polls, native short video, question posts with no link, activity in the first 30 minutes after a post, and a group "quality score" that drops with hidden/reported posts and passive scrolling. Posts with outside links get less reach; links belong in the first comment. Groups are also harder to reach in the app now, so members only see posts that make it into their main feed.

What that means for Mahj Matchmaker: the group fell off because it became a place to browse albums, not to talk, which the ranking reads as low engagement. It can come back partway if posts start conversations: polls, "help me match", "show your table". Those are now drafted for Nov 9-15 (docs/social/fb-group-week-2026-11-09.md) and need nothing from her but a yes. Expect slow recovery (weeks), not a return to hundreds a week; the reliable growth lever stays the email list and the site.
