# MAHJ MATCHMAKER
## The Dating App Comedy Campaign — Creative Brief & Content Idea Bank

### THE BIG IDEA

**It's a MATCH!**

Finding your perfect mahjong match, one swipe at a time.

Mahj Matchmaker is essentially a dating service for mahjong tiles, mats, and racks.

The tiles are single. The mats are looking for love. The racks have commitment issues. Some combinations have undeniable chemistry. Others desperately need counseling.

And Tiffany? She's the Mahj Matchmaker.

She's a creative entrepreneur and graphic designer with a great eye for color, a direct personality, and a dry sense of humor.

She takes the romantic lives of mahjong accessories *entirely too seriously*.

That's the joke.

The campaign spoofs Tinder, dating apps, The Bachelor, Love Is Blind, relationship counseling, dating profiles, red flags, breakups, and romantic comedies.

Beautiful mahjong combinations meet ridiculous dating humor.

**The personality:** Smart, playful, direct, dry, tongue-in-cheek, and occasionally absurd.

**The visual direction:** Classic, approachable, designer-led, with natural wood, whites, soft florals, blush, sage, autumn tones, and beautiful product photography.

**The approach:** Mostly designed graphics, product footage, and mockups, with occasional face-to-camera comedic appearances by Tiffany.

# PART 1: THE ORIGINAL CONTENT CONCEPTS

## 1. THE MAHJONG DATING APP

**Concept:** Fake Tinder-style dating profiles for mahjong tiles, mats, and racks.

Every tile set becomes an eligible single looking for the perfect partner.

Example:

**Meet Rosie, 28**

Fun, flirty, a little dramatic.

**Looking for:** A mat that can handle my personality.

**Love language:** Coordinating accessories.

**Dealbreaker:** Beige without personality.

**Relationship status:** Single and ready to mingle.

Caption ideas:

- "Single and ready to mingle. Preferably on a mahjong table."
- "Her love language? Coordinating accessories."
- "She's a 10, but she clashes with your racks."
- "Looking for something serious. No casual tile situationships."
- "Swipe right if you believe in love at first tile."

**Execution:** Each new tile album is introduced as a dating profile. Show a few possible matches, include funny commentary, and end with a teaser.

**Launch teaser:** "Your perfect match is coming. 10.27.26."

# PART 7: HOW TO KEEP CONTENT PRODUCTION EASY

The goal is not to become a full-time social media influencer.

The goal is to create recurring, funny content that makes people remember Mahj Matchmaker and visit the website.

### Three Content Formats

**1. Designed Graphics & Carousels — Primary Format**

Dating profiles, red flags, text-message breakups, missed connections, match reveals, and Tinder-style product cards.

No camera appearance necessary.

**2. Hands-Only Product Content & Voiceovers**

Present different mats, tiles, and racks while Tiffany narrates the romantic drama.

Examples include The Rack Ceremony, love triangles, and chemistry tests.

**3. Occasional Tiffany Appearances**

Counseling sessions, confessionals, matchmaking interventions, hotline calls, and introductory videos.

These are special appearances, not a daily requirement.

### Production Philosophy

Build a reusable profile template.

Use existing tile mockups and album content.

Batch graphics whenever possible.

Capture a few funny on-camera clips in one filming session.

Reuse reaction shots.

Keep the comedy natural, deadpan, and conversational.

No costumes, exaggerated personas, or elaborate filming setups required.

Tiffany's personality is enough.
