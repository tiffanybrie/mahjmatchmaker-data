## 5 — What MahjMatchmaker.com does

**Website:** https://www.mahjmatchmaker.com/ (not “MajMatchmaker.com”).

Mahj Matchmaker started in a Facebook group called **“Mahj Matchmaker, Hooking Up Tiles, Mats, and Racks.”** Tiffany made extensive cross-brand graphic mockups of tile sets matched to mats and racks, housed in Facebook group albums. Initial group membership grew 200–300 per week but growth collapsed after ~3–4 months. Members used it as a reference library, consumed answers and left without interacting; many waited for Tiffany the expert to answer questions instead of interacting with each other. Facebook favored active discussion, so the group stopped being recommended; reach and new members declined to about one a day. Some albums required huge labor while creating little affiliate revenue. This was a **platform/format mismatch**, not proof people didn't value the albums.

Tiffany recently moved the albums to her website. There are **37+ curated albums** of tile sets with various mats and racks, including across different brands. These are browsable/static inspiration and direct customers to partner sites. She caught up on missed album releases with Claude; roughly **10 new tile-set albums** are ready to drip.

She is launching **Find Your Match** on **October 27, 2026**, as part of Mahj Matchmaker 2.0. It's an interactive builder embedded on her website, not currently a standalone phone app. It will bring products from partner brands' Shopify catalogs into the tool, including ongoing updates/new products. Shoppers can start with a tile set OR a mat they own, discover matching products from across brands, explore Tiffany's personal recommendations using a **“Tiffany's Matches”** control, and build/share their final combination. The finishing graphic is shareable and carries Mahj Matchmaker branding/affiliate code. Customers click out to external partner sites to buy, and Tiffany may earn affiliate commissions. Brand partnership gives brands distribution/inclusion; Tiffany also sometimes includes nonaffiliate brands she personally loves. She controls brand inclusion/featured content and personal curated recommendations; users can explore their own combinations. It is inspired by retailer-specific “Build Your Set” tools (example: Oh My Mahjong), **but uniquely supports cross-brand discovery** and does not necessarily physically overlay all products in the same manner.

The core customer question: **“What mat goes with these tiles?” / “What tiles go with this mat?”** Customers frequently ask this in mahjong Facebook groups, struggle to envision colors together, and manually screenshot/Photoshop product images. Some love collecting and changing their tabletop frequently. The product aims to make testing and sharing possible without photo editing. Long-run aspiration: become a major authority/resource for coordinating mahjong accessories. Real commercial metrics: visits, tool uses, shares, affiliate outbound clicks, conversions; not just Facebook group likes/comments.
