## Where I'd adjust ChatGPT
1. **"Hideous mismatched mat" photos need a safe villain.** The counseling skit, "It's not you, it's your mat," and Red Flags all need a bad pairing on screen. Per your never-shame rule, the bad mat can never be a real partner product, or anyone's real table. Use a made-up or generic villain:
   - a plain beige felt mat
   - a Christmas mat in March
   - a clashing mat we mock up ourselves with no brand
   
   The joke is the pairing, never the mat.
2. **Twelve recurring series is too many.** Pick 4–5 that can repeat forever (see the content buckets below). Keep the rest as one-offs.
3. **Ages ("Rosie, 28").** Real ages could read oddly next to real products. A playful age works better: "Age: 6 days old (launching this week)", "Age: retired, from the vault" or "Age: fresh out of the box". Vault sets as "the older woman who still has it" is funny.
4. **Love Is Blind's ending, "Until someone called Mahjong,"** is the weakest line. Keep the pods-and-reveal idea, but end on the real match.
5. **"Two posts a week" undersells what we've built.** Planoly auto-posts graphics and silent reels, so posting isn't the bottleneck. Your time is. Plan: 3–4 auto-posted graphic or reel posts a week from templates, plus stories when you feel like it.
6. **ChatGPT doesn't know about Find Your Match data.** Several of its jokes get better with real numbers (see "Wrapped" below).

## Your voice: dry, not sassy
ChatGPT's notes describe you as direct, analytical and deadpan. Not New Yorker, not sweet Southern, not grouchy, no costume. Our doc 63 lines lean sassy ("Listen, honey…", "Don't worry, babe"). Suggested swap:
- Retire: "honey," "babe," "girl."
- Use: "I have concerns." / "Absolutely not." / "I see the potential. I don't see the chemistry." / "I'm going to pretend I didn't see that." / "Oh, she's gorgeous." / "I think we have a match."
- Short, flat delivery, then the beautiful match does the talking.
