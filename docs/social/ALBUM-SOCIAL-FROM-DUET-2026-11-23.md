# How an album is promoted on social (from Duet, relayed by Tiffany, Mon Nov 23 4:28pm). CURRENT; overrides older social docs where they differ.

WHAT WE MAKE: one REEL per album on the Instagram grid (grid posts + reels only). The 3-slide "It's a mat-ch!" carousel (Oct 21 format) is RETIRED unless she asks. Never straight match cards dropped onto a post, never stacked cards, no match card in a story.

ALBUMS: 15+ matches (ideally 20+). Review: one picks pass, then a grid page for final order. Album reel = that brand's products only (so the brand reshares). For an OMM album: the reel uses the "Top Oh My Mahjong matches" section only; the album page also has a cross-brand section (Tiffany 4:24pm).

REEL BUILD (Google Doc "Mahj Matchmaker Reel Build SOP"): open album, cover, colour card, top 10 match cards, Find Your Mat-ch teaser, end card. Opening frame = cover; every reel ships with a real cover image. Alternate formats day to day: cover-first, album flip, swipe deck. Match cards 4:5, labeled, TWO racks always, never edge to edge, never shrunk or cropped close to text, no empty bands, no cards on blurred backgrounds. Most patterned mat first, never two solids in a row. FYM on her real white phone, big, real swipes; search types the BRAND name, then taps the tile set. Safe zones: no text at the very top/bottom, don't shrink to the middle. End card = the POSTED Christmas FYM reel: 6 staggered logo rows, same speed, no visible repeats, every partner somewhere, featured brand's logo showing, Oh My Mahjong always, Mahjong Register off, held ~7s (v4 waiting on her OK). Approved stays approved. Start from her approved reels as templates (Dear Santa FYM-first vB). No blue/navy with holiday. She adds the music and posts.

CAPTION: one copy-paste block: hook line / "Comment MATCH for the link to the album and Find Your Mat-ch" / "<Brand> discount code: MAHJMATCH" / 3-4 hashtags. Never say she's excited about a product.

SCHEDULE: 12 holiday albums in grid order (Dear Santa, Rackin' Rudolph, Nutcracker, Holly Jolly Red, Sugarplum Pink, December 1994, Tinselle Emerald, Nivalee or Tinselle Ruby, Grinch Green, Tinselle Ruby, Spirit of Christmas, Apres Ski); no two snow albums side by side at the end. Virora reels wait until Dec 1 (code off during its sale): never post a brand's album while its code is off. "12 albums of Christmas" rerun starts Dec 12.

WHERE: reels + covers in Duet's Mahj Social channel folder reels/<album>/; Album Playbook + holiday pick sheet in project doc "00l ALBUM HANDOFF". Anything needing her goes to her MAIN chat.
