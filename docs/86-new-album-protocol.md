# 86 New Tile Set Album Protocol: color card FIRST (Oct 19)
Tiffany, Oct 19, after Papaya Spritz: "We need to establish the protocol for a new album and make sure that we follow it next time." The Papaya album got built before anyone had a close look at the tile photos or Oh My Mahjong's description. This is the order from now on. Read alongside doc 77 (The Gut Check) and doc 76 (Color Theory).

## The order (no skipping steps)
1. **Wait for the brand's product page.** No match album until the tiles are on the brand's own website, especially Oh My Mahjong, since their launches come so often. Teasers and Instagram stories aren't enough to build from.
2. **Read the brand's product description and save their color words.** For example, OMM on Papaya: "papaya orange, soft lavender, and fresh green." Our public copy never contradicts the brand's words.
   - Also save any "behind the tiles" layer graphic when the brand posts one (OMM sometimes adds it after launch).
3. **Close-up photo review, before any matching.** Zoom into every product photo: backs, each layer, faces, jokers, dragons. Also sample the layer colors from the photos (Claude can measure hue and lightness) and compare them to the brand's words.
   - Watch for brightened brand photos. If the tiles are in hand, the real tiles win.
4. **Color card first.** Backs, layers and faces, using the brand's color name, with our nuance in small text where it helps (e.g. "Lavender · a cool, blue-leaning lavender"). Tiffany checks it.
5. **Only then build the album and pick matches.** We match to the photos (and real tiles when we have them), not to a description. The color card is what keeps the copy and the matches consistent.
6. **Then the profile, social, Facebook group and Find Your Match entry**, all reading from the same color card.

## Papaya Spritz check (Oct 19)
Measured from OMM's 7 product photos plus our tile photo:
- **Purple layer:** median hue about 248° (range 239–265°). True lavender sits around 275°, periwinkle around 240°. So it's a **soft, blue-leaning lavender**, closer to periwinkle than a classic lavender. OMM calls it "soft lavender."
- **Green layer:** hue about 80–88°, light and soft (≈ #b5ca82). That's a **light lime / fresh green**, not a bright lime. OMM calls it "fresh green."
- **What we published:** the album page says "lavender and green layers" and the color card says Lavender + Lime, so nothing contradicts OMM. "Periwinkle" appears only in mat names and the profile line about the mats she's seeking.
- **Matches:** unchanged. Periwinkle mats still work because the layer leans blue.
- **Small copy tweak to consider:** color card "LIME" layer → "LIGHT LIME" or "FRESH GREEN".
