## Content timing rules

- **restock known date**: Tease 7-10 days before (pick your mat now + album link + MAHJMATCH), reminder 1-2 days before or the day the brand announces the exact drop time, launch-day story with link, one follow-up 2-3 days later only if still in stock.
- **restock window**: Build the content now but HOLD it. Schedule the tease the day the brand announces the exact date (email/IG). Never spend the 'it's back' content before the item is buyable.
- **restock no date**: Hold all content; watch weekly. Post only when a date appears.
- **new launch album set**: Existing launch rules (swipe book / album); post on launch day.
- **affiliate only info**: Never post: brand asked affiliates to keep it quiet.
- **no commission**: Content value only (no code). Lower priority than partner drops on the same day.
- **travel mini**: Skip (we never match travel/mini tiles to full-size mats); travel-tiles blog article only.
