# Our Facebook group: posting strategy (Tiffany Nov 12 10:40am, BINDING)

"If you just are always posting daily, I think this is part of how I killed my group." Groups are for engagement, not a news feed.
- **No one-off news posts.** No "Charleston launched", "now this brand launched" posts. Each one gets tiny engagement and trains people to skim.
- **Outside links go in the COMMENTS, never the post body.** Links that send people off Facebook downgrade the group.
- **The weekly brand post stays** (Monday codes + updates): long, one-stop shop. It will become the Brand Watch post.
- **Optionally, a "what's going on in the world of mahj" roundup a couple of times a week**, built for engagement: ends with a question ("What's on your mahj wish list?"). Later: "Just added to Brand Watch" style.
- **Every post is approved by Tiffany first.** No autonomous "engaging content". She has limited bandwidth; the group is NOT a top priority right now. Nice if it grows on its own; don't push it ahead of her priorities or flood her with yes-asks.
- Group = our own Mahj Matchmaker group. (Posting intros in OTHER groups is a separate plan: config/fb-groups.json.)
