### F2. Mat door, tile deck: the price filter does not put unpriced sets last

**Affected:** both copies, both widths.

**Repro:**
1. Open the mat door.
2. Search "lotus cove" and pick Lotus Cove.
3. Set Price to "Under $175".

**Result:**
- The deck order runs `$$$$$-$-$$$$-$$$-$-$-$$$…`. Taylor is 6th, Dandy 8th and Emerald 13th, and 31 priced sets come after the first unpriced one.
- The note under the dropdown says "9 without a listed price shown last", which is false.
- Screenshot: `shots/site_390_m05b_tile_price_not_listed.png`. The 6th card is "Taylor · Sold out · Price not listed".

**Cause:** the `setsForMat` wrapper sorts priced-first inside her/album/catalog, but `tAll()` then re-sorts album+catalog by `TSOM` (D.tso), which undoes it.

The mat deck (tiles door) is correct: unpriced mats come last, no priced mat is over the limit, and the card says "Price not listed". Gate [166] tests PRF only and misses this.

## Notes

- **Z (zoom swipe).** Swiping while zoomed works, but at 2.56x from a centred zoom the finger has to drag past the photo's edge first: about one card-width (about 370px on the phone, about 500px on desktop) before the card starts to move. A quick flick swipes from anywhere. This matches the spec in zoomswipe_v118.py. My first fixed 380px drag on desktop did not reach the edge, and the retest with a longer drag passes (`zt.js`). Tiffany may still find the slow drag long. A flick is the natural gesture.
