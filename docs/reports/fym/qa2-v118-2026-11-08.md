## Failures

### Q1. Artifact slims a partner brand's mat: Fait à Main "Texas Mon Amour Chocolate Blue"

- `config/fym-partner-coverage.json` lists Fait à Main as a partner brand.
- `config/autoapply-hosts.json` records it as "partner (UpPromote, approved Oct 20) but MAHJMATCH is not applicable at its cart".
- Because the code does not apply at its cart, its mats carry `pa: false`.
- `artifact118.py` picks slim candidates with `not o.get('pa')` (line 96), so it treats "no auto-apply code" as "not a partner".

**Effect:** in the artifact, `faitamainstudio.com/texas-mon-amour-mat-chocolate-blue` is `hid` + `ahid: 1`. It stays visible on the website copy and in the build.

**Repro:**
1. Open `fym/pub118/index.html`.
2. In `D.mats`, look up the key above: it has `ahid: 1`.
3. In the app, open the mat door and search "texas mon amour". Peach, Cream and Green show, but Chocolate Blue does not.

**Fix:** exclude partner-coverage brands from `CANDS` (Fait à Main, and any partner whose code is not set up). Slim one more non-partner mat instead, if the space is needed.

The other 38 are non-partner brands: Bougie Mahjong (dead links), Betty June & Jeannette, Mahj & Moon (non-matched), Piper Chase Studios, Rebecca Illustrated, Southern Living, Case-Mate and Amazon ShopMy picks.

**Note:** the earlier slimming of 238 Let's Be Besties and 4 Noli June mats (partners, `SLIM_BRANDS`) is pre-existing and by design. It is not new in this delta.

### Q2. Mat door "See all" grid: unpriced sets shown before priced ones when a price is chosen

**Repro (both copies, both widths):**
1. Open the mat door.
2. Search "lotus cove" and pick Lotus Cove.
3. Set Price to "Under $175".
4. Tap **See all**.

**Result:**
- The first section, "Best color matches", holds 9 sets, and every one says "Price not listed": Taylor, Dandy, Emerald, Birdie, Sorbet, Glitterville, Limoncello, Lola, Sunset.
- The priced sets come after them, in "From our partner brands" (24, all priced). That section sits behind the "Show partner brands' tile sets" button.
- The dropdown note above still says "9 without a listed price shown last".
- Screenshot: `qa2/shots/site_390_p_grid_175.png`

**Same pattern at every price option** (`$` = priced, `-` = unpriced):

| Price | Best color matches | From our partner brands |
|---|---|---|
| Under $175 | `---------` | 24 priced |
| Under $300 | `---------` | 24 priced |
| Under $350 | `$---------` | 24 priced |
| Under $400 | `$×21 ---------` | 24 priced |

**Why the gate passed:** inside each section the order is correct (priced first). That is what gate [166b] and the release notes test. Across the grid, though, priced sets follow unpriced ones. With a price chosen, Lotus Cove's See all view opens on a wall of "Price not listed".

**Fix options:**
- With a price chosen, move unpriced album sets below the partner-brand section (one "Price not listed" group at the end), or open the partner section automatically.
- Either way, extend [166b] to check across sections.

### Q3 (NEW, FAIL): a price filter makes the app say "Tiffany hasn't matched this mat yet" on mats she did match

**Repro (both copies):**
1. Open the mat door.
2. Search "lotus cove" and pick Lotus Cove. Her picks are Juniper and Talulah, both $475, or $427.50 with code.
3. Set Price to "Under $175" (or $400).

**Result:**
- The deck subtitle reads "Tiffany hasn't matched this mat yet, so the closest color matches come first."
- The See all grid heading reads "Tiffany hasn't matched this mat yet. These tile sets are the closest color matches."
- With Any price, both correctly say "Tiffany matched … with 2 tile sets".

**Why it is false:** she did match this mat. Her picks are only over the price limit.

**Cause:**
- `TGG` sets `herN = g.her.length` from `setsForMat()`, which has already removed her over-limit picks. So `herN` is 0, and the release note's "her header still counts all her picks" does not hold.
- The tswipe subtitle uses `g.her.length` and has the same problem.

**Scale:** a data estimate counts the matched mats whose every pick is over the limit. That is 334 of 464 at Under $175, 306 at $300, 283 at $350 and 85 at $400.

**Fix:** count her picks before the price filter. When they are all over budget, say for example "Tiffany matched this mat with 2 tile sets (over your price)". Then add a gate check.

This filter is new in 1.18.
