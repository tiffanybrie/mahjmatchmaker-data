## Question
**What's the difference between Dapper 1 and Dapper 2?**

## Answer (2–3 sentences)
Oh My Mahjong has made two versions of its Dapper mat, and both are now retired. **Dapper 1** is the original, a browner, olive-toned plaid; **Dapper 2** is the newer version, a more burgundy plaid. When you're hunting for a retired Dapper, check which one you mean: the brown one or the burgundy one.
