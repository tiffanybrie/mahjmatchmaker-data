## (a) The strategy in 5 bullets

1. **One message, timed to the buying window.** Every piece ends at Find *Your* Mat-ch™, the albums or the code. "Pick your mat now" teases go out 7 to 10 days before a dated restock, and the "it's back" post goes out the day the set is buyable. Sold-out sets with no date (Coquette, Gatsby, Drake, La Fete, Tortoise, Sunday premium mats incl. Rattan) stay held.
2. **Sell holiday in November, not December.** Holiday starts Nov 1, peaks the weeks of Nov 1 and Nov 8, turns into gift guides Nov 15, and Black Friday codes Nov 23 to 30. December is a light last call, then evergreen.
3. **Push OMM hard until Nov 25.** OMM pays 20% until then, and its moving sale runs the first week of November. OMM sets that are in stock (Heritage 2.0, Pippa, Smith, Papaya Spritz) and OMM Christmas mats get the most feed spots until Nov 25.
4. **Reuse before we build; Planoly carries everything.** The countdown, 3 holiday tables, more than 400 holiday match cards, 45 drafted "It's a mat-ch!" picks, 13 Season 2 episodes and 30+ scheduled pins already exist. She posts only reels with her song and link-sticker stories. Everything else auto-posts after one weekly yes.
5. **Brand reshares are the free growth.** Every post tags every brand shown, using the verified handles in `config/social/ig-handles.json`. Small partner brands reshare readily, and the holiday reel tags 15 of them.
