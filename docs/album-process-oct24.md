# 86b Album Process update (Oct 24) - read with doc 86

## Oct 24 update (Tiffany, voice note): the color card comes BEFORE the swipe, every time
Missed on Papaya and again on Brownstone and Gatsby: the color card was made after her picks. From now on **no swipe book goes to her without the color card at its top** (tile photos + the colors), so she understands the set before she matches it.

**How to read a tile set's colors (her method):**
- Back color = dominant (on every tile). Layer colors = next (on every tile, seen from the side).
- Face colors count by how many tiles carry them: bams, cracks and dots (36 each) dominate the table; dragons (12-16), winds (16), flowers (8) and jokers (8-10) matter much less. A color that's only on the winds is there, but it won't drive the match.
- **Top matches pick up several of the set's colors** (back + layer + the main suit colors). A mat that only repeats the back color matches, and can be included, but it won't be a top match.

**How the swipe book shortlist gets built:**
1. Color wheel pull from the whole mat library (gallery + Find Your Mat-ch), weighted as above.
2. **Claude's by-sight pass** on the pull: look at every mat next to the tiles and cut what's clearly wrong (a secondary color only, a room photo misread, etc.).
3. **Start from her own matches of similar sets** (Drake started from Heritage's matches; a berry-layer set like Deco Mulberry starts from Lagoon's/purple sets' matches).
4. **Outside research:** Tiles & Tablescapes FB group (what members suggest for that set; label "TNT rec"), other mahjong groups, Google Images. TNT suggestions also surface brands we don't carry: list them for affiliate outreach.
5. Keep the book a sensible size (Coquette-size beats 113): best candidates first.

**New products are not albums (Oct 24):** a new mat goes straight into the mat gallery + Find Your Mat-ch (color tagged) so every swipe book can pull it. New products are tracked for social: a weekly "new this week" roundup, and better, "coming next week" so content lands at launch, on the social calendar. An album is built only when it earns it (e.g. Bam Bird Deco relaunch + 3 matching Deco mats).
