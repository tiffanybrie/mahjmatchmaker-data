# Find Your Mat-ch reels: how to show the app (STANDING, Tiffany Oct 27 10:23am)
Read before building ANY reel, carousel or story that shows the FYM app. Written after the Oct 27 launch reel (Sunday x Brownstone Bloom) went out "just okay".

## Show the app the way a user feels it
1. **Labels and stickers sit ON the thing they name.** A "Sunday tiles" label goes by the tiles at the top, never over a mat. Every callout lands where its product is on screen.
2. **Swipes move.** ♥ = the card flies RIGHT and drops into the suitors row at the bottom (show it landing). ✕ = the card flies LEFT and is gone. The stamp lands on the card being swiped.
3. **Count every product on screen.** If a mat is visible in the deck (Oct 27: Ozark Mahjongg Carson Vintage was the first card under Sunday), it's part of the story: heart it, show it fall to the suitors, name it, tag it. Non-partner is fine. More brands on one card = the cross-brand story. Oct 27 actually had FIVE brands; we said four.
4. **"Zoom" means zoom (Tiffany Oct 27 1:06pm, said many times): in the RACKS step, tap the + on the mat preview above the racks (plus, plus, plus) and pan to the pattern so the mat is seen up close while racks are swiped; the zoom stays on as each rack is hearted. That's the point: from the far aerial view you can't judge a green rack against the mat. Zooming the deck card in the mats step is a bonus, not a substitute. Capture: assets/social/restock-oct27 (08-rz*, 09-rk* frames).** Earlier wording: When the copy says the mat is zoomed, actually use the app's zoom (+) and show the camera getting close to the mat while racks are picked. Don't say a feature; show it.
5. **Show the features, not just the flow:** suitors filling up at the bottom, zoom on the mat in the racks step, filters (colour, brand) changing the deck, the match card, shop list with codes / "No code available".
6. **Camera moves.** Push in on the part of the screen being used (button, filter, suitor row), pull back out for context. Interactive menu changes. Never one static phone frame for the whole reel.
7. **Energy:** match the Oct 26 "We've been busy matching" reel: hype, boom-boom-boom pacing, where we've been / where we're going. No quiet stretches.

## Process
8. **Watch it as a user before sending.** Check every frame against rules 1-7, not just "nothing overlaps". The QA agent gets this file.
9. **Finished the evening before, with time for her notes.** She has no time to edit on post day (day job). A rushed post-day render is a miss even if it posts on time.
10. When the match uses a set or mat, look at what else the app shows for it and suggest it ("Ozark Carson Vintage is a suitor here, want it in?") instead of only what was asked.
11. **Centred means centred (Tiffany Oct 27 10:27, recurring).** Every graphic and reel runs `python3 scripts/social/center_check.py <file>` before she sees it; any band off by >16px is fixed or justified (a deliberate side callout). Known causes so far: fixed-width text columns laid out from the left (Oct 27 2am intro, COL_W), ™/italic overhang counted in the centred box, and half-banners designed left-aligned for a split grid.
12. **Know the brand first (Tiffany Oct 27 12:07).** Before making ANY content featuring a brand, read its last ~12 Instagram posts (Planoly get_instagram_account_posts, workspace 019fc27c-…) and note what it's pushing right now and what it pairs its products with; use those pairings/launches first. Notes live in docs/brand-social/<brand>.md (the partner watch updates them).
13. **Restock playbook:** brand story/post with link -> we reshare + "shop <brand> with code MAHJMATCH" -> our frame(s) show the APP: "Mock up <set> with all N of her matches in Find Your Mat-ch". Never a feed post; never match cards alone.

## Tinder vibe + the full experience (Tiffany Oct 27 2:05pm, after the Jade how-to)
14. **Show BOTH ✕ and ♥, every step.** Mats: at least one ✕ (card flies LEFT) between hearts. Racks: SWIPE racks the same way, at least one ✕ and two ♥; never let a rack just "pop up" as if it's the only option (the Jade reel ended on a Bird & Bamboo pink rack with no rack swiping shown).
15. **Show the choices:** colour filter (tap a colour chip, deck changes), rack type chips (Wood / Clear / Pearl / Tortoise...), brand filter. The viewer must see there's a whole menu to pick from.
16. **Full flow in order:** tiles → swipe mats (✕/♥) + colour filter → suitors → racks step: + + + zoom on the mat → filter rack type → swipe racks (✕/♥) with the mat zoomed → match card.
17. **Tinder vibe, not full skit:** big ✕ / ♥ buttons, cards that tilt as they're swiped, a ♥ or ✕ stamp on the card, an "It's a match!" moment styled like a dating app. Lighter than the Smith "tile sets dating" skit, but unmistakably swipe-dating. Never "nope/crush/pass" words (UX rule 7): just ✕ and ♥.
18. **Racks must match a colour you can SEE in the zoomed mat (Tiffany Oct 27 4:42pm).** Measure the mat's colour on the zoomed frame and pick the rack that matches it (Evergreen & Tartan red = Bird & Bamboo Red, not Wine). Don't copy album rack pairings blindly.
19. **Match card = the tall Story 9:16 card (Tiffany Oct 27 4:43pm), full size on screen.** Never the squat Post 4:5 crop. pk/shot2.py FMT=Story saves the full-res card (<out>_full.png, 2160x3840).

20. **THE PHONE NEVER MOVES ON A SWIPE (Tiffany Oct 29 11:32am).** The launch reel ("ever-burning question", Oct 27) slid the WHOLE PHONE left/right instead of swiping the mat card. That is wrong every time: the phone frame stays fixed; only the card inside the screen tilts and flies off (✕ left / ♥ right, stamp, next card underneath), like scripts/social/reels/how_tinder.py. Check before sending: compare phone-frame bbox across frames of each swipe scene; any movement of the frame = fail.
21. **Every brand shown is named + tagged (Oct 29).** Ozark was on screen in the launch reel and missed (no heart, no label, no tag). Before sending, list every product visible on any frame and confirm each has its label/tag.
22. **SHOW WHAT WE'RE REALLY DOING: tiles AND mat together, big (Tiffany Oct 29 11:35am).** On the Jade reel the tiles were a sliver at the top of the frame above the mat, so viewers couldn't tell we were matching tiles to a mat. Every swipe frame must show the TILE strip and the MAT clearly together (crop/scale the phone so the tile strip is large and readable; if the app layout puts tiles out of frame, zoom the shot to the tiles+mat area or add a tile inset). Check before sending: on each swipe scene the tile strip occupies a clearly visible band (not cut off, not a sliver), and a viewer could name the tile set from the frame.

## Her reel vision, learned the hard way on the holiday intro (Nov 1-3, 5 rounds). Check EVERY reel against this list before she sees it.
23. **Length fits the song.** Ask/know the song first; the reel ends where the song phrase ends (holiday intro: 40-43s for All I Want for Christmas Is You). Never cut a song off mid-line.
24. **Open with the brand + an exciting beat, 2-3s.** We're a new brand: say "Mahj Matchmaker" / Find *Your* Mat-ch™ big up front (e.g. the logo putting on its holiday look). Never a flat statement ("It's holiday time." with a period); never cheesy props (a plain box "present").
25. **Show DEPTH, not 3 thumbnails.** A count ("140+ mats") gets a full wall of them tiling out to the edges. Edge-to-edge is GOOD for walls of real photos; what's bad is blurred-background photo dumps "smashed into view".
26. **Never a photo with white space around the mat.** Trim/crop every mat photo; reject if the mat doesn't fill the frame.
27. **Introduce the tool before the tutorial.** Many viewers see Find Your Mat-ch for the first time: say what it is ("Mix and match them all, plus the tiles you own") before any app step.
28. **Show where we are before zooming.** Each app step first shows the screen in context (what screen, what's being picked), then pushes in on the button. A tight crop of half a mat with no context loses people.
29. **Step text: short, no periods, ad style** ("Pick your mat", "Swipe holiday mats"); only the opener may carry "!". Don't be literal ("Zoom in on your mat" -> just SHOW the zoom). Text sits in the light space around the phone, never over the app's top bar.
30. **Instagram safe zones on every frame:** nothing important above y 250 or below ~y 1500 (caption), nothing in the right-side buttons (x > ~920, y 950-1500). Run an overlay check frame by frame before sending.
31. **Sharp app footage.** Capture at 2x (Chromium --force-device-scale-factor), mat photos at full size (never the app's 140px fallback); look at a 100% crop before sending.
32. **White phone** (real iPhone frame), never the black bezel. Light snowy blue + snow for holiday (no navy); red accents, navy text.
33. **Swipe enough:** 3+ hearts so the favorites/pick screen shows real choice; use the clearer "Which suitor is the one?" screen, skip duplicate small frames.
34. **Racks:** at most one painted colour, paired with wood / pearl / acrylic (more brands than just Bird & Bamboo); no orange at Christmas; go beyond her picks with colour theory.
35. **Cross-brand only for the tool.** Match cards that sell Find Your Mat-ch are the APP's cross-brand cards, never single-brand album cards. Logos next to "use code MAHJMATCH" = true code partners only.
36. **Keep the camera moving** (push-ins, ending push on the match card); always end on the brand end card (name, across brands, code pill, Link in bio, a ticker of many partner logos, never a few that make us look small).
37. **Camera never parks (Tiffany Nov 3 2:32pm, repeated: "Don't just slap up a picture and leave it there statically").** In app scenes the camera moves the WHOLE time: push in on each action (button, card, swipe, chip), drift/pan while holding, pull back for context between steps. One push-in per reel is not motion. Reference: the Malachite how-to (scripts/social/reels/how_tinder.py, assets/social/restock-oct27/how/).
38. **Text never overlaps text** (plates vs app text, plates vs card text). Push-ins on a card stay gentle: every word of the card stays in frame.
39. **Fill the frame:** no dead empty bands (e.g. logo rows sitting high with blank space under them: add a row, re-space). Things hidden behind a counter box are the least important items (e.g. tile sets with no album), never the ones being showcased.
