# Find Your Mat-ch reels: how to show the app (STANDING, Tiffany Oct 27 10:23am)
Read before building ANY reel, carousel or story that shows the FYM app. Written after the Oct 27 launch reel (Sunday x Brownstone Bloom) went out "just okay".

## Show the app the way a user feels it
1. **Labels and stickers sit ON the thing they name.** A "Sunday tiles" label goes by the tiles at the top, never over a mat. Every callout lands where its product is on screen.
2. **Swipes move.** ♥ = the card flies RIGHT and drops into the suitors row at the bottom (show it landing). ✕ = the card flies LEFT and is gone. The stamp lands on the card being swiped.
3. **Count every product on screen.** If a mat is visible in the deck (Oct 27: Ozark Mahjongg Carson Vintage was the first card under Sunday), it's part of the story: heart it, show it fall to the suitors, name it, tag it. Non-partner is fine. More brands on one card = the cross-brand story. Oct 27 actually had FIVE brands; we said four.
4. **"Zoom" means zoom.** When the copy says the mat is zoomed, actually use the app's zoom (+) and show the camera getting close to the mat while racks are picked. Don't say a feature; show it.
5. **Show the features, not just the flow:** suitors filling up at the bottom, zoom on the mat in the racks step, filters (colour, brand) changing the deck, the match card, shop list with codes / "No code available".
6. **Camera moves.** Push in on the part of the screen being used (button, filter, suitor row), pull back out for context. Interactive menu changes. Never one static phone frame for the whole reel.
7. **Energy:** match the Oct 26 "We've been busy matching" reel: hype, boom-boom-boom pacing, where we've been / where we're going. No quiet stretches.

## Process
8. **Watch it as a user before sending.** Check every frame against rules 1-7, not just "nothing overlaps". The QA agent gets this file.
9. **Finished the evening before, with time for her notes.** She has no time to edit on post day (day job). A rushed post-day render is a miss even if it posts on time.
10. When the match uses a set or mat, look at what else the app shows for it and suggest it ("Ozark Carson Vintage is a suitor here, want it in?") instead of only what was asked.
