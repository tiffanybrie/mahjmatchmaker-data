## 1. Who we are

Mahj Matchmaker (mahjmatchmaker.com, @mahjmatchmaker) is Tiffany's mahjong matchmaking brand. We hook up tile sets with the mats and racks that bring out their best colors. It started when Tiffany made tile, mat and rack mockups for her own table. That grew into a Facebook group, then a library of match albums on the website, and now **Find *Your* Mat-ch™**, the tool that lets shoppers match their own tiles. The brand plays tongue-in-cheek dating app: every tile set is an eligible single, and the Mahj Matchmaker sets them up. Under the jokes, the matches are real and color-true, and every one of them is Tiffany's own pick. The business runs on affiliate income from the code **MAHJMATCH** and partner links. The #1 goal is to be the answer on Google (and Google's AI answer) when someone searches "[brand] mahjong discount code" or "what mat goes with my tiles". We talk to collectors: of course you need the new tiles, and of course you need the new mats.

## 3. Voice & the character

### The Mahj Matchmaker (the character)
- **The Mahj Matchmaker is a slightly dramatic, nosy, self-appointed dating expert.** Bossy but loving, and she **always ends by helping**: the real match is the payoff.
- **Tile sets, mats and racks have dating personalities.** A tile set has a profile, a type, a love language and dealbreakers.
- **Classy dating language only.** Never name a dating app or show (no Tinder, Hinge, Bumble, Bachelor or Love Is Blind; formats can echo them, names can't). No innuendo beyond the running **"nice rack"** joke.
- **Never knock a real product or a partner brand.** Say dealbreakers helpfully ("Keep the mat clean and saturated"), never as an insult. Never shame anyone's matches or real tables. No "red flag" call-outs of how people match: mahj is an open world, and Tiffany's rack picks are her taste and tips, not rules.
- **Bad pairings use safe villains only:**
  - the recurring **beige felt mat** ("the beige boring mat"), always the same asset (§7)
  - a possible "hideous mat" spoof that we design ourselves, with no brand
  - "a Christmas mat in March"
- The joke is the pairing, never a real mat.
- **Collectors, not problems.** We play up the collector ("No, you do have a problem. But you're in very good company."). Never imply a bold or specific set is a bad buy. Never joke about real diagnoses (OCD, addiction).
- **Never make it about her.** No self-deprecation. Don't repeat bio facts (e.g. OMM instructor) in captions. **Never invent backstory.** The true origin: she made mockups *for herself*.

### Genders
- **Tile sets get a gender.**
  - **Male:** Malachite, Heritage (and Heritage 2.0), Smith, Cypress (Cypress Forest), Gatsby, Drake.
  - **Female:** Jade, Solara, and Papaya Spritz (already published as "her").
  - **Any other set:** ask Tiffany once before writing a pronoun into content. Keep working on other items while you wait.
- **Mats and racks stay gender-neutral**, so a pairing never reads as same-sex or opposite-sex. It is not a topic.

### Profile wording (the dating profile)
- Section labels, in caps: **HER PROFILE · HER COLORS · RACKS SHE'S INTO** (HIS for male sets).
- Prompts: **Looking for** / **Love language** / **My most irrational fear**.
- Status line examples: "The new girl on the block". A playful age is fine ("fresh out of the box"). Never use real-person ages.
- **The profile UI shows ONLY ✕ and ♥.** Never star, lightning or rewind. Never "crush", "nope" or "pass" anywhere (UI, captions or reminder boxes).
- Retired Papaya answers never come back: "long-term mat partner", "coordinating racks", "beige tablecloth". `content_lint.py` fails on them.

### Lines that work (approved or posted)
- "Mahj Matchmaker Hotline, how can I help? 📞🍹"
- "A party girl tile meets a party girl mat" (Tiffany's own edit)
- "You deserve someone who brings out your *best colors*."
- "Hook me up!" (a brand slogan)
- "50 matches and counting"
- "See all of Pippa's matches" (doorway framing, never "your one match")

## 4. Captions by channel

### Rules for every caption
- **Short, funny, relevant.** It should sound like she typed it on her phone. Not overworked, not blog-style. Instagram and Facebook captions stay **≤350 characters before the hashtags** (`content_lint.py` enforces this).
- **The hook's job is to land the piece.** It says what the video or graphic doesn't say outright. The hotline reel never says "Papaya is calling the hotline", so the hook does: "Mahj Matchmaker Hotline, how can I help? 📞🍹".
- **No colour theory on funny pieces.** Colour theory lives in the albums. On social, use it only when the piece itself is about colour. No rack lists and no match lists in captions.
- Sound genuinely excited, never "meh". Never add a call to action that doesn't follow from the piece.
- **Hand captions over as one final copy-paste block** that she doesn't have to edit.
- **Disclosure goes in captions only, as #affiliate**, the last hashtag. Never write "I may earn a commission" on a visual or in a caption. (On the website, one quiet footer line covers the whole site.)
- **Launch times are in Central time only**, e.g. "Wed 10/21 at 10am CT". Never add Eastern or other zones.
- **Counts:** use the exact number from the live album page, then "and counting". Never round up, never phrase a count as final ("Nearly 40" reads like a closed book), and never write the number **404**.
- **Brand tags:** every brand shown or named gets its **verified** @handle from `config/social/ig-handles.json`. Never guess one. If a handle is missing, read it from the brand's website footer and add it first. Put the main brand in the caption; tag the rest in Tag people.
- Every reel says **link in bio** (reels can't hold links).

### Instagram feed post (incl. "It's a mat-ch!" and profile carousels)
The format, in this order:
1. The hook, with the set's own emoji
2. The set + brand @handle(s) + "link in bio"
3. "Code MAHJMATCH 💗"
4. 3–5 hashtags, in the caption (not the first comment), ending with #affiliate

> It's a mat-ch! 🍹💗 A party girl tile meets a party girl mat.
> Papaya Spritz by @ohmymahjong + Heiress by @brittmillsdesign, link in bio
> Code MAHJMATCH 💗
> #mahjong #mahjongmat #ohmymahjong #mahjmatch #affiliate

### Instagram reel
Use the same format. The hook names the scene.

> Mahj Matchmaker Hotline, how can I help? 📞🍹
> Papaya Spritz by @ohmymahjong has 50 matches and counting, link in bio
> Code MAHJMATCH 💗
> #mahjong #mahjongtiles #ohmymahjong #mahjmatch #affiliate

- Every reel reminder lists **3–5 song options with start points**: love songs or songs that fit the vibe. She picks.
- **Music goes in through the Edits app at the right timestamp.**
  - If the reel has built-in sound (e.g. the hotline ring and elevator music, which ends at about 0:28), her song starts where that audio ends.
  - Silent reels: the song starts at 0:00.
  - Never tell her to add the song with Instagram's music button. It lays the song over the whole reel and buries the built-in sound.
- **Never use library or royalty-free music** (Planoly's or Instagram's).
- The **album cover is the reel's grid cover**.
- A reel revision always comes to her as one complete, swap-in video. Never pieces to splice.

### Instagram story
- **One product combo per frame**, and each frame tags only the brands on it. A mention can be hidden: add the @mention, shrink it, and tuck it behind the image. It still notifies the brand.
- Plain image frames with no sticker auto-post from Planoly. Frames that need a link, countdown or poll sticker, or a brand mention, are reminder posts for her.
- Never put a song on one frame of a multi-frame story.
- Sharing a reel to her story carries its music ("share to story" toggle).
- Frame text example: "She's live! 🍹 Papaya Spritz just dropped. Her album's ready: 50 matches and counting." The link sticker goes on this frame.

### Pinterest
- Pins are SEO copy. Lead the title with the search phrase. The description can run to 500 characters (Pinterest's limit).
- Frame a pin as a doorway to all of a set's matches, never "here's your one match".
- A styled pin shows one tile set + one mat + one rack set + accessories.
- The kicker on the image reads "shop the look · mahjmatchmaker.com".
- Boards: see `config/pinterest/queue.json` (e.g. "Pink Mahjong Tiles and Mat Matches", "Christmas Mahjong Mats", "Amazon Mahjong Finds").

### Blog
- Use first person, in Tiffany's warm, knowledgeable voice. Keep it evergreen: no "as of October 2026" unless the post will be updated.
- Product links go straight to the product page with affiliate tracking, never to a brand homepage.
- Lead with what she has matched, and link the albums.
- Give firsthand recommendations only on products she has used. Never claim hands-on experience she doesn't have. Never imply she has reviewed every brand's mats.
- Balance placements. No brand gets three detailed placements; a one-line mention covers the rest.
- Some ideas belong only on the blog, such as the "marry the versatile one, date the mats" buying advice.
- Never spell out or universalize her matching method.
- Holiday tile sets are never "play year-round".
- Never post a full, unwatermarked view of a brand that watermarks its photos (e.g. Mac + Miller).

## 8. Process checklist: before anything ships

**Before you draft**
1. Read this guide. Read the set's live album, profile JSON and `ig-handles.json`.
2. Build **one example first** and get her sign-off before making the full set.
3. Use only her real picks, in the live album order. Never invent pairings, racks or colour claims. Non-partner brands only if allowed (CLAUDE.md, `builder/fym_flags/nonpartner_allow.json`). No Mahjong Studios mats. Holiday means only Christmas, Hanukkah and winter/ski scenes.
4. Gender check (§3). If the set isn't listed, ask once and keep working on other items.

**Before you show her**

5. Run `python3 scripts/social/content_lint.py <captions>`. It checks for banned words, other codes / TEACHMAHJ, old profile answers, icons and length.
6. Check every image yourself on a contact sheet:
   - readable at phone size
   - logo legible
   - safe zones clear (`render.py` prints `OVERFLOW`)
   - no halos
   - no brown
   - ✕ and ♥ only
7. Counts match the live album. Times are in CT. Only verified handles are used.
8. Anything you ask her to review must open with one tap: inline images, playable video, working links. Test it first.

**Sweep rule**

9. **When something changes in one piece, sweep every draft and finished piece for the same change before anything goes out.** That covers racks, profile card, buttons and wording.

**Posting**

10. **Planoly carries every post.** Every approved post goes into Planoly at its posting time, the day it's approved. Check `list_post_groups` before telling her what to post. Chat never replaces Planoly.
    - **Auto-post (after her yes on that post):** feed images, carousels, plain image story frames with no tag or sticker, pins.
    - **Reminder posts for her:** reels with a popular song, and story frames that need a link, countdown or poll sticker, or a brand mention.
    - The reminder caption opens with **"🛑 TIFFANY: DELETE THIS BOX BEFORE YOU POST 🛑"** and spells out, tap by tap:
      - song + artist + start point in Edits
      - cover
      - Tag people handles
      - stickers
      - order and time
      - "delete the box, then share"
    - The box ends with a line of em dashes; the caption follows.
    - Never turn on "Also share on Facebook" (it only offers her personal profile).
11. **Final launch check** before anything goes out. Run through it all again:
    - names and ™
    - code, and no other codes
    - counts
    - times
    - handles
    - music plan
    - Planoly slot and type
    - disclosure (#affiliate in the caption, nothing on the visual)
12. For anything that touches Find Your Mat-ch: run `python3 builder/fym_gate.py <build_dir>`, then independent QA. She is never the QA team.

**Colour, catalog and partner rules (summary; the details are in CLAUDE.md)**
- Only partner brands, ShopMy mats and the mats she explicitly allows come into the gallery or Find Your Mat-ch. Never pull a non-partner's whole line because one mat matched.
- Colour sections sort by her lead calls, then measured share. Album colours are hints, never added where a mat doesn't visibly have them.
- Holiday mats live only in Holiday, except for the two approved exception lists.
- Her colour calls always win and are locked.
