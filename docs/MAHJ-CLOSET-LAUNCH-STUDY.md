## Why theirs looks good
- The screen fills the whole frame (no phone bezel); when they pull back it floats as a rounded card over a blurred copy of itself.
- ONE feature per reel. The big preview stays put; each tap visibly changes it.
- Slow pace, calm holds, minimal serif type, lots of white.
- Recurring voice: "Gina" humor, Clueless closet story.
- Best performer = the founder telling WHY she built it.
