## The Mahjong Line: her note checks out (for the program they promote)

Her note (Oct 31): "I think will only allow you to affiliate if you are in their teacher program which requires exclusivity." That's correct for the only program the site promotes.

- **The TML Ambassador program is for instructors only and is exclusive.** It's linked in the footer, and the page (https://themahjongline.com/pages/become-a-tml-ambassador) says:
  > "We love growing our mahjong community and continue to recruit mahjong instructors (AKA: TML Ambassadors). We selects ambassadors to be exclusive TML representatives in many regional areas. We limit the number of ambassadors in a given area to avoid market saturation."
- **The application form says it outright.** The Typeform (https://ox8u47p4pr2.typeform.com/to/rBWiKxlH) contains:
  > "We require our ambassadors to be exclusive to The Mahjong Line."

  It also asks:
  - "How committed are you to being an exclusive ambassador with The Mahjong Line?"
  - "Are you currently an ambassador/affiliate with any other mahjong tile companies?"
  - "What makes The Mahjong Line the brand you want to represent exclusively?"
  - "Do you use The Mahjong Line tiles to teach your mahjong lessons currently?"
- **No general affiliate signup is public.**
  - The store runs GoAffPro (its loader is on every page) and has a portal at https://the-mahjong-line.goaffpro.com/.
  - No page on their site links to that portal, and /pages/affiliate(s) returns 404.
  - So the public way in is the exclusive ambassador route, which she can't take with about 30 other brand programs.
  - The only possible opening: her universal GoAffPro login might list The Mahjong Line in its store directory. Not checked (it needs her sign-in). It stays LATER either way.
- **Contact if she ever wants to ask:** connect@themahjongline.com
