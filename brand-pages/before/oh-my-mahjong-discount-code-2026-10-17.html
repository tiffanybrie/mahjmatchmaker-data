Why Oh My Mahjong is special to me

Hi, I’m Tiffany.Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor and the matchmaker behind Mahj Matchmaker.

Oh My Mahjong blends traditional American mahjong with a modern, design-forward look, and it’s one of the most popular places to buy a first (or fifth!) set.

As an Oh My Mahjong Mahji Mentor instructor, I have a personal love for this brand. They were my very first introduction to mahj and my first set of tiles (I love you forever, Sierra!), and they’ve supported my teaching journey in the best way.

Oh My Mahjong is also where Mahj Matchmaker began. Most of my tile match albums started with Oh My Mahjong sets, so you’ll see their tiles and mats on nearly every page of this site.

— Tiffany, Mahj Matchmaker

My favorite Oh My Mahjong tiles

It’s hard for me to pick my favorite OMM tiles because I love so many! Heritage is probably my favorite set because it’s incredibly versatile, as are its brother Smith and its sisters Glow Wild and Talulah, both with tortoise backs. Patterned-back tiles are your friend when it comes to matching lots of mats!

Questions about Oh My Mahjong

Is there an Oh My Mahjong discount code?

Yes. Code MAHJMATCH saves 10% on eligible Oh My Mahjong products. Enter it in the discount code box at checkout. If it doesn't apply, the item may be excluded or already on sale.

Which Oh My Mahjong tiles are the most versatile?

Patterned and natural-texture backs match the most mats. My favorites are Heritage, Smith, Glow Wild, Talulah and Pearlescent. See the most versatile mahjong tiles for more.

Does Oh My Mahjong sell travel mahjong sets?

Yes. Oh My Mahjong carries travel-size tile sets in addition to full-size sets.
