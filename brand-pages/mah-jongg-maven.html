About Mah Jongg Maven

For more than 28 years, Mah Jongg Maven has made engraved and hand painted Mah Jongg sets that keep the traditional suits everyone knows, with color and personality layered on top. They built the business through a printed catalog long before online shopping, and today offer more than 30 tile sets and colorways.

Their best sellers are the 3 layer Sparkle sets and the sets with jewel-tone ink in pink, turquoise and royal blue. They also make custom Joker tiles for many sets, and their newest collections are artist-inspired, like Mahj in Bloom with artist Lorra Kurtz. You’ll find mats, racks, cases and accessories there too.
