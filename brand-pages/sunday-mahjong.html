Questions about Sunday Mahjong

Is there a Sunday Mahjong discount code?

No. There is no Sunday Mahjong discount code at this time, and Sunday Mahjong isn’t a Mahj Matchmaker affiliate partner. Codes you see on coupon sites may not work. I’ll update this page if that changes.

What color are the backs on the Sunday tile set?

Warm butterscotch backs with ivory faces. The artwork is by illustrator Josh Avren.

Does Sunday Mahjong make a Christmas tile set?

Yes. December 1994 is their holiday set, with deer, poinsettias, cardinals and a nutcracker joker.

What mats go with Sunday Mahjong tiles?

Sunday’s own mats are a natural pair, and muted, vintage and moody mats in mustard, ochre, deep green, navy and plaid look great with the Sunday set. My Sunday album shows my favorite matches across brands.
