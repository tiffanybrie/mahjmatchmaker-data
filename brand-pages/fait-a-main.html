About Fait à Main

Fait à Main is a creative design atelier in Houston, Texas. Founder Luisa Rangel de Alba studied textile design in Mexico City, Florence, Milan and San Francisco, and learned the hand-painting technique of marquage during years in luxury retail with the French maison Goyard. That hand-painting is the studio’s signature.

Their mahjong mats are printed with Luisa’s original art on leatherette with a soft slip-resistant backing, most at 31.5" square, and nearly every mat can carry a monogram hand painted in the studio. Prints include the Texas Mon Amour bandana collection, Provençal stripes and florals, the Studio Print line and Le Drip. They also make scalloped acrylic racks, painted leather pouches, totes and needlepoint. Free standard shipping on US orders over $250; monogrammed pieces are final sale.
