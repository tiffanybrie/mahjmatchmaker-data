About Spools

Spools started with Keen, its maker, and a grandmother who couldn’t carry her 14-pound mahjong set anymore. Keen is a mechanical engineer who designed hardware for rockets, aircraft and iPads, and set out to make a set that stays full size on the table but is light in your bag.

Every tile, rack and pusher is designed and 3D printed in the USA and made to order. Tiles, racks and pushers weigh about 3 pounds together and pack into one tote, and the tiles float, so a set can go to the pool or the beach.
