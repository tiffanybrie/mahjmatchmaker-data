About YOLO Mahjong

YOLO Mahjong was founded by a Singaporean couple with a shared love of Chinese culture, storytelling and mahjong, a game their grandmothers played. Each collection is built around a story, starting with Chinese literature, and their logo is four interlocking hearts, one for each player at the table.

Their first storytelling set is Journey to the West: 160 larger-format tiles (about 1.5" × 1"), engraved and UV printed on ivory fronts with ivory marble-look backs, with a matching neoprene mat, dice tray and 20" tortoiseshell or gold racks sized for American mahjong. They also make a big range of printed 31.5" mats (florals, chinoiserie, tropical, ripple and scallop borders, holiday plaids) and personalize tile backs for individual orders. Shipping is free on US orders over $50.
