About Mahjong by SI

Mahjong by SI is the mahjong mat line from Stacie Ivers Francombe, who grew up hearing her mom’s weekly mahjong group shuffle tiles down the hall. Her Atlanta print and fabrication company, Signs by SI, printed custom mahjong mats for about five years before Mahjong by SI had a name, and her husband and three kids are part of the business.

The designs come from an in-house designer and collaborations with independent artists, and every mat is printed to order in their U.S. facility. Natural rubber mats come in 20", 31.5" and 33.5"; the SI Signature faux leather mats come in square, scalloped and round shapes. They also offer personalized and fully custom mats, and new designs drop every Mahjong Monday.

Questions about Mahjong by SI

Is there a Mahjong by SI discount code?

Yes. Use code MAHJMATCH at checkout on mahjongbysi.com for 10% off your mahjong mat.

What is Mahjong by SI known for?

Printed mahjong mats, with hundreds of designs from an in-house designer and artist collaborations, printed in the USA by its sister company Signs by SI in Atlanta. New designs drop every Mahjong Monday.

What sizes and materials do Mahjong by SI mats come in?

Natural rubber mats come in 20" travel, 31.5" and 33.5". SI Signature faux leather mats come in 20" travel, 33" square, 33" scalloped and 34" round. Both use a 3mm construction.

Can I get a custom Mahjong by SI mat?

Yes. You can personalize one of their designs with a name, monogram, logo or text, or start from a blank mat in their Custom Mat Builder and upload your own artwork.

Are the mats made to order?

Yes. Every mat is printed to order in their U.S. facility, and most orders are final sale because they are made to order.
