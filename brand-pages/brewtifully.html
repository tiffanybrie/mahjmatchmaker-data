About Brewtifully

Brewtifully is the work of Tracy Dawn Brewer, a real working artist: a surface pattern designer and painter who has sold her own patterns for more than a decade. Every mat starts as her hand-drawn or hand-painted artwork, from folk-art florals to painted landscapes and holiday scenes. She taught art at the Canton Museum of Art in Ohio, paints murals and now works from her studio in Seattle.

Her mats are made to order, so you choose the design, size and thickness. Her art also shows up beyond her own shop: a 57-mat collection with Mahjong by SI, and Miss Mahjong’s Deep Green Garden Party mat, reproduced from one of her paintings.

Brewtifully mahjong mats

A satin-smooth finish. Her SleekSurface mats have a smooth, satiny fabric surface over a rubber backing, made in the USA, and it’s a finish people really like at the table. She also offers satin-smooth neoprene mats with stitched edges.

Every size, and a price for every budget. Mats run from 20.9" travel mats through 26", 31.5" full size and 33", with a 32" round on some designs. Rubber-backed mats start around $45 and SleekSurface mats are $95. Many come in 2mm or 3mm: the 2mm is the budget-conscious choice, but it’s thinner and more prone to wrinkling, so choose 3mm if you want it to lie flatter.

Who makes Brewtifully mahjong mats?

Tracy Dawn Brewer, a surface pattern designer and painter. Every Brewtifully design starts as her own hand-drawn, hand-painted artwork.

What sizes do Brewtifully mahjong mats come in?

From 20.9" travel mats through 26" and 31.5" full size up to 33", with 32" round on some designs. Many come in 2mm or 3mm thickness.

Should I get a 2mm or 3mm Brewtifully mat?

2mm is the budget-friendly, lighter choice but is more prone to wrinkling. 3mm lies flatter and cushions the tiles more.

What is a SleekSurface mat?

Brewtifully's SleekSurface mats have a smooth, satiny finish over a rubber backing, made in the USA. They ship rolled, so unroll and let them lie flat for a few minutes.

Does Brewtifully make custom mahjong mats?

Yes. Tracy makes custom mats for players, clubs, organizations and events, and her art is in a 57-mat collection with Mahjong by SI.
