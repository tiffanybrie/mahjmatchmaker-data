About Brewtifully

Brewtifully is the mahjong line of Tracy Dawn Brewer, a surface pattern designer who has sold her own patterns for over a decade. Every design starts as her own hand-drawn, hand-painted artwork (folk-art botanicals, chinoiserie gardens, Roaring ’20s art deco, prairie cowgirls and playful holiday characters), with no AI art.

Pieces are made to order, so you pick the design, colorway and size, from travel mats to 33" full size. Tracy also makes custom mats for clubs and events. Her artwork appears in the Brewtifully x Mahjong by SI collection, and Miss Mahjong’s Deep Green Garden Party mat comes from one of her paintings. She taught art at the Canton Museum of Art in Ohio and now works from her studio in Seattle.
