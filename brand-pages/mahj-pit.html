About Mahj Pit

Mahj Pit started because its founders couldn’t find tiles that matched how they wanted to play: not disposable plastic, and not another set covered in palm trees and preppy patterns. So they made design-conscious tiles in translucent acrylic that catches the light, built to be played hard and passed down.

Their best-known set is the Original Confetti tiles, with an iridescent confetti finish and a high-contrast blue face that’s easy to read across the table. They also make low-pile chenille and neoprene mats, travel mats, 20" acrylic racks with magnetic pusher storage, dice holders and vintage finds for the table.
