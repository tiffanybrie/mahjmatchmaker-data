# ShopMy setup plan (Oct 18) — for Tiffany's approval

## Can My Shop use her OWN affiliate links? YES (with a caveat)
- ShopMy's "Finalize and add" product editor has an editable **Destination Link / "Add a custom link here"** field. Pasting her own affiliate URL (tested with Mahjong Row's `?sca_ref=12475363...`) keeps her sca_ref intact in that field.
- Those products show "This link won't earn commission" — meaning ShopMy pays nothing, but the click goes to her URL, so the brand's own program (UpPromote/GoAffPro) still credits her. ShopMy's help docs don't address custom affiliate destinations explicitly.
- So My Shop can be one complete storefront: ShopMy-paid links where ShopMy pays (OMM 8%), her direct affiliate links for partner brands, and Amazon links once her tag is saved.

## What ShopMy actually pays on (tested Oct 18)
- Oh My Mahjong 8% — the only partner where ShopMy is useful (OMM's own program is code-based, so these links earn on clicks).
- Bam Bird Boutique 4% (her direct link pays more — use direct).
- No commission: Mahjong Row, Luxe, Sunday, Hip Hip, Middle & Main, Peace Love, Britt Mills, Mahj Squad.
- Amazon: no commission until her Amazon Associates tag (heritagesig05-20) is saved in ShopMy Account Settings. **Needs her OK** (account setting). Then pasted Amazon URLs auto-convert.
